# Cookie

A petite black cat with green eyes. Doesn't age. Her shadow doesn't quite match her body. In Agnes's care — nominally.

## The surface

By every visible sign, an ordinary if slightly odd house cat. This page is deliberately structured surface-first, true-nature-second, because that's the order the crew themselves learned it in, and because most of the cast's day-to-day interaction with her is still with the surface version.

## True nature

An eldritch entity that spent ten years disguised as a cat before the crew learned otherwise. Her mouth is wide enough to swallow a person, and contains voids and stars. She hunted Sable and Aldric as prey through the streets in that decade, and won, before being told to stop. See `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for their side of that history.

**The void.** Behind her mouth is a liminal space — a quiet wood under too many stars, centered on a small wooden cabin. Inside it, her true form is a secluded woman with long black hair and bright green cat-like eyes, who makes soft plush dolls, content and alone, a basket of leftover scones and cat treats beside her. She talks to the dolls and dotes on them.

**Being swallowed is not necessarily fatal.** What the doll-maker does to what she takes in is remake it. See "The Holy Rot / Cookie's Void," below, for the one time this has been shown on the page.

**How she decides.** She doesn't explain, doesn't warn, and doesn't ask. The crew can't predict her and has no idea what her ceiling is. This is treated consistently across arcs as a fact about her, not a mystery the crew is expected to solve.

## Mr. Patterson

For reasons she's never disclosed to anyone, Cookie was an injured kitten on an ordinary patch of grass when Mr. Patterson found her and took her in. Ten years followed. He's the first person she ever bonded with, and she keeps his one rule — no eating people — out of love for him, not obligation or fear. It's the only known constraint on her, it's voluntary, and it doesn't extend to anyone else. See `arcs/cookies-true-nature.md` for how he died and what he asked of Agnes and Cookie both.

## Arc appearances

**Cookie's True Nature** — Mr. Patterson's ghost brings her to the bakery after his death, asking Agnes to care for her. That night, a hungry Cookie reveals her true nature, hunting Sable and Aldric; his ghost catches her and gives her the no-eating-people rule that's held ever since. See `arcs/cookies-true-nature.md`.

**The Holy Rot / Cookie's Void** — The canonical reveal of what the void actually is. Faced with an incurable holy-energy poison killing Sable and Aldric by slow accumulation, Cookie eats them without warning or explanation, remakes them in the void to strip the rot out, comes near to keeping them, and releases them a week later on the grounds that they're still needed out there. Neither ghost remembers any of it. This arc also carries Agnes's full week of grief at what she reasonably believes is a loss — day one denial, day two bargaining (Cookie's uncharacteristic "cuddly era," a deliberate comfort measure rather than an admission), day three a real cry, day four back to work. Cookie knows the whole time they're coming back and doesn't correct her. See `arcs/the-holy-rot-cookies-void.md` and `characters/agnes.md`.

**The Lich's Wheel** *(alt-timeline)* — Watches the wheel activate and pull the crew through, and goes back to sleep. Not among those transported.
