# Historical Cast

All long dead. Included because their history underpins present-day arcs.

## The chapterhouse (Origin arc)

**Pell.** Senior archivist. Took in a ten-year-old Sable after catching her stealing from the kitchen, and four years later took in Aldric, sole survivor of the regional fever, raising him the same way: gentle, unhurried, no pressure to talk before he was ready. Put both children's sharp minds to work in the archive. One of the two who set up the sealing ritual. Sent south to an archive after the collapse; died there of old age, still trying to preserve a record of what happened.

**Tem.** Close friend of Sable and Aldric, six years younger than Sable, effectively a younger sister to her. Fiercely loyal and willing to act on what she judges important. The other of the two who set up the ritual. Went into hiding rather than take a posting after the collapse, and spent twenty years working to free Sable and Aldric. Discovery would have meant being locked up. Died not long after succeeding, her body giving out from the toll of it.

**Maren.** Prior. Released the Hunger by accident, poisoned Sable to keep it quiet, went on performing corrupted passing rites rather than admit either, and sabotaged the sealing ritual to kill everyone who had uncovered it. Exiled afterward to somewhere cold and quiet, without trial or charge, and lived out her days there.

**The Hunger.** The ancient entity sealed beneath the chapterhouse, released by Maren, resealed by the ritual that killed Aldric. Its dimension held Sable and Aldric for twenty years. Its essence is what keeps them coherent. (Not known in-world.)

## The Aura Chronicle (~420 years before present)

**Aura.** Aldric's aunt; youngest of Osric and Agatha's eight children, born a generation behind her oldest sister Marta. A genuine sensitive from early childhood (saw and spoke with minor nature spirits, could coax plants to grow), which marked her a suspected witch in a devout village. Shielded for years by her siblings, she left at sixteen to escape the church's suspicion and to free them of the burden. Settled alone in the northern hills. Befriended, named, and entered a permanent blood pact with Lamby, which marked her a warlock in the church's eyes for life. Mother to Nettle. Sole ancestor of every present-day Fischer.

Believed for the rest of her life that her entire birth family died in the fever. Learned only secondhand that a young cleric named Aldric had died in a ritual the church covered up. Never learned he survived as a ghost.

Her relationship to the church never resolved into a single clean verdict. Raised devout, she never stopped half-missing the sense of community it gave her parents. The turn against it was personal, not ideological. The same institution that drove her out also took in her orphaned nephew with real gentleness (Pell) and later used him as an unwilling sacrifice and lied about it. She held gratitude and grief simultaneously without needing one to cancel the other out.

Died peacefully at 86.

**Lamby.** An ancient nature deity, roughly lamb-shaped. Held the same stretch of hills and suppressed the pestilence entity there for longer than any human settlement in the region. Neither good nor evil, and drawn to human company, which it finds interesting. Named by Aura as a joke about mortal audacity, which it was amused enough to keep permanently.

Entered the permanent blood pact during the pestilence's escape, protecting Aura and Nettle's land at the cost of diminishing itself permanently into the body of an ordinary lamb. Has stayed with Aura's descendants ever since, present at nearly every birth, wedding, and burial for four hundred years. Known to the living Fischers only as "the old lamb," its nature and its name unknown to any of them. Still suppressing the entity in the present day, on a fragile balance.

**The pestilence entity.** Ancient, native to the northern hills, indifferent rather than malevolent, closer to weather than to a monster. Held down by Lamby for centuries, escaped catastrophically once (the fever that killed Aldric's family and most of the region), later re-suppressed and sealed into a cave barrier keyed to Aura's bloodline. Still stirs.

**Nettle.** Aura's daughter. Real if weaker sensitivity from age four; could hear Lamby faintly and befriended it in her own right. Married into a neighboring family at 22; her descendants are every present-day Fischer. Died decades after Aura, of old age.

**Joel.** Nettle's husband, a local man. Knew enough of Aura's history from Nettle to recognize danger when a militant cleric came asking after her, and ran a shortcut to warn the homestead ahead of him.

**Marta.** Aldric's mother, Aura's oldest sister, and effectively Aura's day-to-day parent given the age gap. Closest of the siblings to Aura after the exile, maintaining secret correspondence for years. Rode alone into the hills, pregnant, to be present for Nettle's birth. Died in the fever with her husband, using her last strength to tell twelve-year-old Aldric he had an aunt somewhere north and west, without further detail.

**Osric and Agatha.** Aura's parents, shepherds. Both died of old age before the fever.

**Wendel, Bram, Corwin, Halvard, Tobin, Garrick.** Aura's six brothers. Organized with Marta as children to shield Aura from the village and the church. Bram carried secret correspondence throughout her exile. All died in the fever.

**Father Ansel.** The village priest. Suspicious of Aura from her childhood, reported her to the chapterhouse. Died among the first adults taken by the fever.

**Joran.** A hunter, lost in the hills and taken in injured by Aura. The two were involved for a season. Left without a word the morning after witnessing a small working, and was never traced. Nettle's father, though he never learned Aura was pregnant.
