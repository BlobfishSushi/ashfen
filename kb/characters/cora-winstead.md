# Cora Winstead

Architect; the crew's born medium.

## Background

Co-founded an architecture firm with her best friend of fifteen years, Daniel. A born medium with innate spirit perception, hidden from everyone except Daniel until her own case forced it into the open. See `world/perception-tiers.md` — Cora sits at the top tier, seeing all spirits easily, above even a sensitive like Agnes.

## Appearance

36. Red hair in a bob, freckles, light skin, blue eyes, delicate features. Average height. Usually neutral-faced, with eyes that are always scanning the room.

Dresses one of two ways: professional (suit jacket and slacks) or casual (bomber jacket and jeans) — nothing in between.

## Current role

Her architecture work regularly puts her inside old, condemned, or otherwise significant buildings — which is how she ends up flagging cases to the crew (the Morrison Building, the house that becomes the Wishing House) before anyone asks her to.

## Arc appearances

**Cora Winstead — The Frame-Up** — Her introduction. Business partner Daniel was murdered by his mentee Peter after discovering embezzlement; Daniel's ghost warned her and named Peter, and she was arrested three days later. Leo took the case; in the interview cell, her attempt to fake not seeing Sable and Aldric failed and she came clean about being a medium. This is also the arc where Chen first realizes something supernatural is going on around Leo. See `arcs/cora-winstead-the-frame-up.md` and `characters/chen.md`.

**The Morrison Building / The Serpent** *(shelved — no longer part of the active story)* — Would have alerted the crew to a medium convention in a building she'd condemned years earlier, haunted by a shadow-serpent invisible to hunters. See `archive/the-morrison-building.md`.

**The Alegrove Island Murders** — Cast as the Secretary (spring-pole snare, boathouse path; invitation name GERALD SPENCE). See `arcs/alegrove-island-murders/overview.md` and `arcs/alegrove-island-murders/roster.md`.

**The Wishing House** *(arc-conditional)* — Room 0 is hers: the house's first-ever taken wish, at six years old, was hers — not to be able to see — granted across the whole building to everyone inside for the duration of the arc. She's also the one who lands the two arguments that finally move Tobias to leave. Full account at `arcs/wishing-house/rooms.md`.
