# The Bakery Crew

The standing cast, present across most arcs.

## Sable

Ghost, 400 years, former cleric-researcher.

**Background.** Grew up on the streets, nameless. Caught at ten breaking into the chapterhouse kitchen; Pell took her in rather than turning her out. Sixteen years in the chapterhouse mellowed her considerably, but the fight-for-survival instinct never left, however well hidden.

**Personality.** Calm and steady, rarely angered and terrifying when she is.

**Ghost origin.** Died at 26 of what she believed was fever, never knowing Maren had poisoned her. Maren then performed the passing rites over her body, sending her to the Hunger. On the other side, Sable recognized the afterlife was wrong, the rites were wrong, and the rites she herself had performed over other people's dead had been doing this to them. That is the source of the fury. Three days crawling back out on it. Aldric was the first person she encountered afterward.

**Ghost category.** Reads cleanly as category 3 (strong emotion). What actually holds her coherent is the Hunger's own essence. (Not known in-world.)

## Aldric

Ghost, 400 years, former cleric-archivist.

**Background.** From a small shepherding village in the northern hills, unnamed (locals called it "up past the ridge"). At twelve, a fast-moving pestilence swept the region and killed his entire immediate family inside two weeks. He was the sole survivor. Clerics found him and brought him south to the chapterhouse, where Pell took him in.

He has always believed the fever was an ordinary illness, and never questioned it, until the Hans Fischer case reveals it as an entity still active in his family's home region, and reveals his family's death and the present-day Fischer crisis to be the same recurring threat. (See the Aura Chronicle: Aldric does not know his aunt Aura ever existed.)

**Personality.** Easygoing, lighthearted.

**Ghost origin.** Died at the door at 24, giving himself as the sacrifice in Sable's place (see Origin arc). Twenty years trapped in the Hunger's dimension afterward, with Sable.

**Ghost category.** Fits none of the three categories. Nobody has ever pushed on it. What actually holds him coherent is the Hunger's own essence. (Not known in-world.)

**Hunger residue.** Aldric's twenty years in the Hunger's dimension left something dormant in him, confirmed exactly once as the catalyst behind the pocket reality in the Echo State/Spider arc. Separate from the essence that stabilizes them both, though presumably related. Deliberately unresolved; a Chekhov's gun with no fixed answer. (Rook and Mara's private instinct that the ghosts' strangeness traces back to the Hunger's dimension is, unknown to them, correct on both counts.)

## Sable and Aldric together

**Relationship.** Platonic partners for four centuries. The Dream Eater incident added a layer of closeness; reality pulled them back afterward, but the partnership sits deeper now.

**Day to day.** Guide spirits, work cases (invisible scouts, access to unreachable places, historic and lost knowledge) and otherwise sit at their corner table or watch bread bake.

## Agnes

Baker. Descends from a bakery Sable and Aldric frequented during Sable's cleric days. A sensitive: a triggering event (the salt-round incident with Rook) sharpened her sight. Sees Sable and Aldric easily, other ghosts with effort. Serves as the ghosts' front and their bridge to the living world. Holds custody of Cookie.

## Leo Castello

Former cop; left the force after digging into a cold case without authorization. Runs private investigations now, with a reputation for solving strange ones. A great detective who brings a cop's instincts to dangerous cases.

Carries a replica banishment bell. Carrying and ringing it case after case knocked partial sensitivity loose in him: he hears and sees Sable and Aldric, less clearly than Agnes does.

Functions as a hunter under Mara's jurisdiction without formal training, a status acquired by accident during the Marlowe case.

## Rook

Hunter, frequent partner of Mara. Brings muscle and initiative. Terse and gruff. Partial sensitive: sees and hears Sable and Aldric directly, less clearly than Agnes.

**After the Echo State/Spider arc.** Carries an unprovable instinct that whatever sets Sable and Aldric apart from other spirits traces back to Aldric's twenty years in the Hunger's dimension. No precedent exists to confirm it. Does not discuss it outside Mara. It has moved his private read of the ghosts' danger ceiling upward without changing day-to-day trust at all.

## Mara

Hunter, senior in the city. Brings strategy and cohesion. Terse and gruff. Partial sensitive. Knows every hunter in the city. Both she and Rook learned about the ghosts and became secret allies; most of the network still does not know, since working with what hunters are meant to banish is controversial.

Same post-Spider instinct as Rook about the ghosts' nature.

## Cora Winstead

Co-founded an architecture firm with her best friend of fifteen years, Daniel. A born medium with innate spirit perception, hidden from everyone except Daniel until her frame-up case brought it out.

## Amelie and Victor (the twins)

Age 14. True Seers: a bloodline ability to see the future, sometimes skipping generations. Their parents do not know. Involuntary visions since seven, covered socially as narcolepsy. Visions show danger (car accidents, fires, heart attacks) and always come true.

Warning people directly never worked, so they document instead: Amelie draws what she sees (visual only), Victor writes what he hears (auditory only). Both catch flickers and whispers outside full visions.

**Friends.** Sarah and Matt, childhood friends, who have noticed the twins acting differently since the bakery entered the picture.

## Chen

Detective on the force, Leo's friend. The only person both aware of the supernatural and inside the department. Knows Leo works with ghosts, without the details. Large Chinese family; occasionally invites the whole bakery crew, ghosts included, to family events.

## Cookie

A petite black cat with green eyes. Does not age. Her shadow does not quite match her body. In Agnes's care.

**True nature.** An eldritch entity that spent ten years disguised as a cat, mouth wide enough to swallow a person, containing voids and stars. Hunted Sable and Aldric as prey through the streets and won, before being told to stop.

**The void.** Behind her mouth is a liminal space: a quiet wood under too many stars, centered on a small wooden cabin. Inside it, Cookie's true form is a secluded woman with long black hair and bright green cat-like eyes, who makes soft plush dolls, content and alone, a basket of leftover scones and cat treats beside her. She talks to the dolls and dotes on them.

**Being swallowed is not necessarily fatal.** What the doll-maker does to what she takes in is remake it. Sable and Aldric were swallowed and remade to strip the holy rot out of them, kept a week, and released. She came near to keeping them and let them go because they were still needed out there. Neither remembers any of it.

**How she decides.** She does not explain, does not warn, and does not ask. The crew cannot predict her and has no idea what her ceiling is.

**Mr. Patterson.** For reasons she has never disclosed, Cookie was an injured kitten on an ordinary patch of grass when he found her and took her in. Ten years followed. He is the first person Cookie ever bonded with, and she keeps his wishes (no eating people) out of love for him, not obligation or fear. It is the only known constraint on her, it is voluntary, and it does not extend to anyone else.
