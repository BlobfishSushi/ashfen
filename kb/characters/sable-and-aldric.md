# Sable & Aldric

Joint page. Their histories are inseparable: same chapterhouse, deaths thirteen days apart, four hundred years as partners, same anomalous ghost category.

## At a glance

|  | Sable | Aldric |
|---|---|---|
| Died at | 26 | 24 |
| Role in life | Researcher, archival branch | Archivist, archival branch |
| Personality | Calm, steady, rarely angered; terrifying when she is | Easygoing, lighthearted |
| Origin | Streets, nameless. Caught at ten stealing from the chapterhouse kitchen; Pell took her in. | Northern hills shepherding village (unnamed; locals called it "up past the ridge"). Sole survivor of a regional pestilence at twelve; found by clerics, brought south to the chapterhouse. |

Both were scholar-clerics at the Dawnchurch chapterhouse, archival branch, not frontline fighters. Bell-trained (all clerics were, regardless of branch); they can ring the true bell correctly, though not to a ritualist's standard, and it is dangerous to them. See `world/objects.md` for the bell and `world/church-history.md` for the Dawnchurch.

## Appearance

**Sable, living.** Dark, straight hair, usually worn in a braid over one shoulder or half up. Green eyes, sharp features, sharp gaze. Average height, on the thin side — slightly underfed. Pale.

**Sable, as a ghost.** Reads as recently deceased: blown-out pupils, glossy eyes, sunken with faint dark circles, somewhat transparent. Wears the brown-and-gray Dawnchurch archival-issue robe, wool.

**Aldric, living.** Brownish-black curls, just long enough to fall over his forehead when he looks down. Gray, soft eyes with creases at the edges like he's about to smile. Slightly underfed, like Sable. Taller than her by a head — average height overall.

**Aldric, as a ghost.** Same ghostly presentation as Sable: blown-out pupils, glossy eyes, sunken with faint dark circles, somewhat transparent. Wears the same brown-and-gray Dawnchurch archival-issue robe.

## Relationship

Platonic for four centuries. The Dream Eater incident added a layer of closeness neither expected; reality pulled them back afterward, but the partnership sits deeper now. See `arcs/the-dream-eater.md` for the incident.

## Sable's past

Sixteen years in the chapterhouse mellowed her from the street kid Pell found, but the fight-for-survival instinct never left, however well hidden.

**Death.** Died of what she believed was fever. She never knew Maren had poisoned her. Maren then performed the passing rites over her body; those rites had been corrupted by the entity Maren released, and every soul given them went to the Hunger. Sable worked it out from the other side: the afterlife was wrong, the rites were wrong, and she had been handed to the thing nobody would admit was loose, by a ritual she had trusted and administered herself. Three days crawling back out on that fury. Aldric was the first person she found afterward, and she ground the anger into a drive to find the truth rather than something that would have consumed her.

For Maren, Pell, and the full Origin events, see `arcs/origin.md`.

## Aldric's past

At twelve, a fast-moving pestilence swept the northern hills, sheep first, then the youngest and oldest, and killed his entire immediate family inside two weeks. He was the sole survivor. He has always believed the fever was ordinary illness, and never questioned it.

In the shelved `archive/the-pestilence-the-lamb.md` material, the Hans Fischer connection eventually reveals this as an entity still active in his family's home region, and reveals his family's death and the present-day Fischer crisis to be the same recurring threat — see `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md`. That arc and its predecessor, The Fischer Family / The Fair, are no longer part of the active story (see `archive/index.md`), so in canon as it stands Aldric never learns any of this; he knows Hans only as a chance lookalike he's fond of. See `arcs/hans-fischer-the-piano.md`, which remains active canon.

Aldric does not know his aunt Aura ever existed; she left the village before he was born and nobody ever told him. Aura is the sole ancestor of the present-day Fischers. Marta's line (Aldric's mother) ended with him. *(Not known in-world.)*

**Death.** Died at the door at 24, giving himself as the sacrifice in Sable's place. Twenty years trapped afterward, with Sable, in the Hunger's dimension. See `arcs/origin.md`.

## Ghost formation

Both default to category 3 (strong emotion) in everyone's assessment, including their own. Sable's fury at her betrayal fits the category cleanly. Aldric fits none of the three standard categories, and nobody has ever pushed on it.

What actually holds them both coherent is the Hunger's own essence, absorbed during the twenty years in its dimension. *(Not known in-world.)* See `world/ghost-mechanics.md` for the three standard formation categories.

## Current role

Guide spirits at the bakery. Work cases with the crew as invisible scouts: access to sealed rooms, unreachable places, historical and lost knowledge. Otherwise sit at their corner table or watch bread bake.

They also help other ghosts cross over — calming a newcomer, explaining what happened to them, and letting them know a door's there to walk through if their business is settled and they're willing. No ritual or object involved, just the standing they've earned from having been dead a long time themselves. It's the closest thing left to their old work as clerics. See `world/ghost-mechanics.md`.

Agnes serves as their front and bridge to the living world. Leo, Rook, and Mara perceive them directly but less clearly than Agnes does. See `world/perception-tiers.md` for the full system and `characters/agnes.md` for the bridge role.

## Vulnerabilities

For the general framework see `world/ghost-mechanics.md`. Character-specific notes:

- **Salt.** A salt round injured both badly enough to force a retreat (the Bakery/Agnes arc). First confirmation.
- **The true bell.** Dangerous to them as well as to its target. They can ring it; they cannot lift it without the emitter.
- **Holy energy (the rot).** Accumulation-based, presents as fever, no known cure outside Cookie's intervention. See the Holy Rot entry below.
- **Blood loss in solid form.** *(Emitter-dependent; see `world/objects.md`.)* Blood is spirit energy made liquid. Massive loss can drop them into an echo state, reliving their deaths on loop.

## Arc appearances

Brief notes on how arcs change their state. Full context on the arc pages.

**The Bakery / Agnes** -- Salt-round injury; first established harm.

**The Dream Eater** -- Relationship deepened; see Relationship above.

**The Echo State / The Spider** *(arc-conditional)* -- Aldric's Hunger residue was the catalyst behind the pocket reality. After this arc, Rook and Mara carry an unprovable instinct that the ghosts' strangeness traces to the Hunger's dimension. They are correct on both counts. Their private read of the ghosts' danger ceiling moved upward; day-to-day trust unchanged. See `arcs/the-echo-state-the-spider.md`.

**The Holy Rot / Cookie's Void** *(arc-conditional)* -- Holy energy accumulated as fever with no known cure. Cookie swallowed them both and remade them to strip it out. A week inside, then released. Neither remembers any of it. See `arcs/the-holy-rot-cookies-void.md`.

**Alegrove Island Murders** -- Cast as the Physician (Aldric) and the Actress (Sable). Their ghost nature is the anomaly that cracks the case: they die on schedule in 1934 but never reach the ruins, and only Leo can perceive their ghosts. The contradiction between Aldric's non-appearance in loops 1-2 and his appearance in loop 3 is the thread that unravels the Physician's faked death. See `arcs/alegrove-island-murders/overview.md`.

**The Wishing House** -- Each gets a room. Aldric's room contains his family alive and relocated west; Sable's contains Pell and Tem alive, no Hunger under the building. See `arcs/wishing-house/overview.md`.

**The Lich's Wheel** *(alt-timeline)* -- Alternate versions exist in a diverged timeline: the Captain (Sable) and the Ritualist (Aldric). Both dead in their own world. See `arcs/lichs-wheel/overview.md`.
