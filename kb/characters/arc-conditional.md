# Arc-Conditional Cast

Only present if the relevant arc is in the active timeline.

## Crypt, CT scans, and emitter

**Dr. Kaia Okonkwo.** Archaeologist, Cordova State University. Excavated the crypt (ASH-17 and ASH-18 are Sable and Aldric's remains). Eventually learns the truth about the ghosts.

**Derek.** CT lab technician. Runs the scans that trap the ghosts in their bones. Terrible taste in music.

**Yuki Tanaka.** Grad student in Kaia's lab. Works out the truth independently, confronts Kaia, joins the circle protecting the ghosts during scans.

**Stephanie Constable.** Signal processing engineer at a defense contractor, Yuki's friend. Decoded the CT interference and built the emitter.

**Mort Voss.** Leader of the mill consortium. Fought the crypt's preservation order. (Distinct from Martin Dormanescu, the twins' father.)

## Morrison Building

**Everett Cross.** Sensitive. Organized the medium convention, manipulated for years by the serpent posing as his mentor.

**Maya Ivanovna.** Genuine medium. Came hoping to switch her abilities off, joined the circle instead.

## Vampires

**Celeste.** Vampire; owns the club Velvet. Took Daniel Vega in after his turning and taught him to feed safely and control his pull. Later shelters non-combatants after the Valeria conflict.

**Julian.** Siren type. Killed club-goers at Velvet by charm through voice, eyes, and touch. Captured after the mission in which he assaulted Sable and Aldric.

**Daniel Vega.** Young hunter turned by Julian mid-investigation. Rescued by Rook and Mara, taken in by Celeste. Later goes undercover in Valeria's coven and becomes a covert operative for Rook and Mara. Officially dead in hunter records. (Distinct from Cora's murdered partner Daniel, who has no surname given.)

**Kira.** Compulsion type. Figurehead of the citywide gathering, fronting for Valeria.

**Valeria.** Ancient stealth type. The real power behind Kira. Planned a coordinated war on the hunter network. Incapacitated by Daniel Vega and taken down.

**Liza.** Young stealth type in Valeria's coven. Frightened, not a willing participant. (Distinct from Elena Dormanescu.)

**Greg.** Turned against his will. Part of Valeria's coven.

**Varick.** Scarred combat type, former soldier. Clocked Daniel Vega as an ex-hunter and kept it to himself. Later joins him as a fellow operative.

**Kai and Jess.** Young hunters captured scouting Valeria's warehouse. Kept alive under partial charm and played along.

**Benny.** The third hunter captured with Kai and Jess.

## Fischer arcs

**Hans Fischer.** 23, music student, northern hills. Descends from Aura, which makes him Aldric's cousin many generations removed rather than a descendant (Aldric's own line ended with him). Knows none of the history until told directly.

**Greta.** 91, Fischer family elder. Practices folk wards, some of which genuinely work. Recognizes that Aldric is not alive and is not dangerous, and keeps his secret.

**David Laskin.** Conservatory student who hanged himself with piano wire sixty years ago. His residual impression haunts the piano Hans practices on.

## Twins' family

**Martin and Elena Dormanescu.** Martin works for the transportation company at the center of the Smuggling Operation, oblivious to all of it. Elena is a fighter pilot. Both unaware of the twins' abilities by default. (Branch-conditional: Elena only knows if she discovers them during the Smuggling Operation. If she does, she approaches the visions as an unfamiliar system to reverse-engineer and builds a framework that helps the twins control and extend them.)

## Standalone

**Horatio Martinez.** Mr. Martinez, a kindly old watchmaker with a small shop; in fact an eldritch being. Reads a person's entire life trajectory at a glance, calculates which memories would improve it by their removal, and builds a bespoke watch to take them. Removed memories are stored rather than destroyed, kept through a door at the back of the shop in a room with no ceiling. Utilitarian, courteous, never wrong by his own accounting, and unmoved by consent arguments, which do not register as an input to what he is calculating. He is not a villain. He read Sable and built her a watch more complex than anything else in the shop. (Not Horatio Bates; earlier drafts using that surname are wrong.)

**Ezekiel.** A werewolf-like boy, briefly taken in by Agnes after wandering into the bakery. (Less-canon branch material; the arc was demoted when the Holy Rot arc took over the Cookie reveal.)
