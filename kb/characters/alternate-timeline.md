# Alternate-Timeline Cast: The Lich's Wheel

Counterparts from a timeline where the Hunger was never released and the eastern chapterhouse became a militant fortress instead of an archive. No Pell, no Maren, no Tem. Both are dead as of the arc's end, in their own world. Kept for callbacks and crossover material.

## The Captain (alternate Sable)

The same person, diverged completely. Caught at ten stealing bread by a drill sergeant, Voss, who saw a weapon instead of a child, and trained accordingly. Youngest captain in the fortress's history at 24: highest mission success rate, highest squad turnover, nicknamed "the mad dog." Efficient, violent, resigned to dying young ("good odds so far" against surviving past twenty-six), trusts only her ritualist.

Met alternate Aldric at fourteen (he was twelve, brought to the fortress as a sole survivor of the same northern-hills fever). He gave her the name "Sable" at sixteen.

Died dragging the lich over the edge of the platform into the void, mortally wounded. Buried a week later by a church search team that never learned what happened in that chamber.

## The Ritualist (alternate Aldric)

Same person, same fever-orphan origin, raised at the fortress rather than in an archive. Trained as a ritualist: reads spirit signatures, wields tuned bells for disruption and detection, keeps a blood-stained field notebook of witness testimony. Twelve years partnered with the captain; he herds, she executes. The only person unafraid of her.

Killed by necrotic-energy injection while holding the battlefield alone. The injection also anchored a fragment of him as a masterless thrall for several days, in which state he tracked down Aura using what present-day Aldric had told him, reached her homestead, and died in her arms moments after she recognized him. Buried near her flock; his notebook and bells are all she has left of him.
