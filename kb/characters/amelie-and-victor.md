# Amelie & Victor

Joint page. Twins, 14. True Seers — a bloodline ability rare enough that it was believed extinct.

## At a glance

|  | Amelie | Victor |
|---|---|---|
| Records visions as | Drawings (visual only) | Written transcription (auditory only) |
| Vision-death state | Blind | Deaf |

Both catch flickers and whispers outside full visions, not just during them.

## Appearance

Same height as each other, for now. Both have large, almost doe-like eyes — made them very cute as small kids, and very unsettling when they're mid-vision.

**Amelie.** Long, slightly curly brown hair down to her back, worn loose. Usually in a red frilly shirt with whatever bottom or skirt she fancies that day. Almost always carrying her sketchbook — see "Records visions as," above.

**Victor.** Short, slightly curly brown hair combed to one side. Thin-framed glasses. Usually in a simple blue shirt with whatever bottom he fancies that day. Almost always carrying his notebook — see "Records visions as," above.

## What Seers are

A bloodline ability to see the future, sometimes skipping generations. Involuntary visions since age seven, covered socially as narcolepsy. Visions show danger — car accidents, fires, heart attacks — and always come true. Warning people directly never worked, so the twins document instead, splitting the sensory channels between them (Amelie visual, Victor auditory).

**Seer lore.** Seers were wiped out roughly a century ago for being too dangerous to warlords and to anyone with plans worth protecting; the last confirmed sighting predates that. Discovery means recruitment or elimination, often both in that order. This is why the twins' status as true Seers, once confirmed, is treated as a real secret rather than a curiosity.

## Arc appearances

**The Twins — Introduction** — Their worst vision yet: the entire school dropping dead simultaneously, themselves included. In the vision-death they woke as ghosts and found Sable and Aldric guiding the dead at the school; Sable offered to help them move on or come to the bakery. They tracked the bakery down afterward, and convinced Leo their visions were real because Amelie had painted Sable and Aldric without ever meeting them. See `arcs/the-twins-introduction.md`.

**The Marcus Webb Case** — Their visions here reveal a deeper rule: visions reflect events that *did* happen in another timeline, sent back for a chance to prevent them — which is how they avoid the deaths Marcus causes them in the vision. Rook identifies them afterward as true Seers. See `arcs/the-marcus-webb-case.md`.

**The Smuggling Operation** *(placeholder)* — Pulled into an investigation of the transportation company their father works for. This is the branch point for whether their parents learn about their abilities. See `arcs/the-smuggling-operation.md`.

**The Mother's Framework / Training** *(shelved)* — If their mother Elena discovers their abilities during the Smuggling Operation, she approaches the visions as an unfamiliar system to reverse-engineer, and builds a framework that helps them control and extend them. Shelved, no longer part of the active story. See `archive/the-mothers-framework-training.md`.

**The Hydra** *(alternate ending)* — If the parents don't find out and the bakery crew is wiped out during the Smuggling Operation, the twins are captured and held by the Hydra syndicate until age 24, becoming feared and wanted criminals before escaping. Filed as an alternate ending rather than a normal branch, since it requires the entire crew's death first. See `archive/alternate-endings/the-hydra.md`.
