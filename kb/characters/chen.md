# Chen

Detective on the force. Leo's friend, and the only person both aware of the supernatural and inside the department.

## Background

Knows Leo works with ghosts, without the details — a deliberately incomplete picture that both of them are comfortable leaving incomplete. Has a large Chinese family and occasionally invites the whole bakery crew, ghosts included, to family events — one of the few places the crew gets to just be around each other socially rather than working a case.

**The name.** His given name is also Chen, distinguished from the surname in Mandarin by tone — not a problem back in China, but one in the US, where the tonal distinction is lost and it just reads as his first and last name being identical. Oddly, it doesn't seem to register as an issue to most people at the precinct. His parents course-corrected with his younger brother, naming him Hugh instead.

## Appearance

35. Cropped black hair, straight, brown eyes. Kind but stern features, sharp jawline. Tall, kind of lanky.

Usually in uniform. Off-duty, he favors a simple t-shirt and shorts.

## Arc appearances

**Cora Winstead — The Frame-Up** — During a car chase after the real killer, Chen sees Leo impossibly know the suspect's route: Sable chasing the car, Aldric shouting directions from above. Works out something supernatural is going on; Leo confirms he works with ghosts, without giving full details. Becomes a trusted contact from this point on. See `arcs/cora-winstead-the-frame-up.md` and `characters/leo-castello.md`.

**The Wishing House** *(arc-conditional)* — Holds the missing-persons report where Tobias's name and photograph have been erased from the file — the record exists, the subject doesn't. The bad feeling this gives him is what sends the case to Leo in the first place. See `arcs/wishing-house/overview.md`.
