# Vocabulary

Canonical terms used across the project. One name per concept, one concept per name.

## Entities and forces

**The Hunger.** The ancient entity sealed beneath the chapterhouse, released by Maren, resealed by the ritual that killed Aldric. Its dimension held Sable and Aldric for twenty years. Its essence is what keeps them coherent as ghosts. Not known in-world.
- Source: Origin arc, world reference

**The pestilence entity.** Ancient, native to the northern hills, indifferent rather than malevolent, closer to weather than to a monster. Held down by Lamby for centuries; escaped once (the fever that killed Aldric's family). Later re-suppressed and sealed into a cave barrier keyed to Aura's bloodline.
- Source: Aura Chronicle, Pestilence/Lamb arc

**Lamby.** An ancient nature deity, roughly lamb-shaped. Named by Aura. Entered a permanent blood pact during the pestilence's escape, protecting Aura and Nettle's land at the cost of diminishing itself permanently into the body of an ordinary lamb. Known to living Fischers only as "the old lamb."
- Aliases: the old lamb (Fischer family usage)
- Source: Aura Chronicle

**Cookie.** An eldritch entity presenting as a petite black cat with green eyes. True form: a secluded woman (the doll-maker) in a liminal void-space. Mouth wide enough to swallow a person; what the doll-maker does to what she takes in is remake it. Keeps Mr. Patterson's rule (no eating people) out of love, not obligation.
- Source: Cookie's True Nature arc, Holy Rot arc

**The placeholder.** The mechanism that runs the Alegrove massacre. Man-height, man-shaped, filled in with nothing. Not a person, not the Physician, not a ghost, not Yates. Can be spoken to; calls itself a placeholder. Courteous, unhurried, entirely without malice. Cannot be eliminated.
- Source: Alegrove Island Murders arc

## Institutions

**The Dawnchurch.** Ran chapterhouses with at least two branches: archival/support (Sable, Aldric, Pell) and militant (frontline, bell-trained, crossed-torch-and-bell insignia). Every cleric was bell-trained regardless of branch. The militant branch retired roughly forty years after Sable and Aldric's deaths.
- Source: world reference, Origin arc

**The hunter network.** What remains after the Dawnchurch's militant branch stopped existing. Remnants of old guilds plus independents. Works like mercenary work, not an order. A senior hunter oversees a city; clusters do not agree on doctrine.
- Source: world reference

**The Office of Lesser Rites.** An Inquisition-style religious sect, called "the Censers" by the hunters who take them down. Deploys the holy rot against ghosts. Name is a placeholder, may change.
- Aliases: the Censers (hunter slang)
- Source: Holy Rot arc

**The back lines.** The structural part of the hunter network: archives, artifact containment, ritual studies, training. They hold the resources and move information between clusters.

## Objects

**The banishment bell (true bell).** The only known genuine banishment bell. Stolen from a museum during Leo's first case, hidden at the bakery. Heavy, two-handed, genuinely banishes. Dangerous to Sable and Aldric as well as to targets. Comes out only when the crew means to banish something that warrants it.
- Source: world reference, Leo Introduction arc

**Leo's replica.** Unblessed iron, right shape, wrong resonance. Stuns and repels, banishes nothing. A tool for buying seconds.
- Source: world reference

**The emitter.** Stephanie Constable's device. Makes ghosts solid, tangible, and visible for a few hours per charge. The hinge for six post-foundation arcs.
- Aliases: solid form (the state it produces)
- Source: Emitter arc

**The Lich's Wheel.** A cartwheel-sized wooden wheel, stone-mounted, bone-inlaid. A lich's escape hatch, a one-way gate for undead energy. Now in the hunter network's vault (Delacroix), flagged never to be read or translated aloud.
- Source: Lich's Wheel arc

## Conditions and states

**Echo state.** A ghost reliving their death on loop, caused by massive energy depletion. Most spirits never come out of it.
- Source: Echo State/Spider arc

**Solid form.** The state produced by the emitter. Tells: dilated pupils, cold skin, no pulse, voluntary breathing that sometimes falls into unnatural rhythm. Dogs sense something wrong; cats don't care. Blood is spirit energy made liquid.
- Source: Emitter arc, world reference

**The holy rot.** A slow-acting holy energy poison. Accumulation-based, presents as fever. No known cure (Cookie's void is the only precedent for removal). Deliberate echo of Sable's original poisoning.
- Source: Holy Rot arc

**The passing rites.** Church rites for the dead, corrupted by the Hunger's release so that every soul given them was delivered to the Hunger. Sable had performed these rites over other people's dead as a cleric. Maren performed them over Sable's body.
- Source: Origin arc

## Perception

**Born medium.** Innate spirit perception (Cora). Sees all spirits easily.

**Sensitive.** Perception sharpened by a triggering event (Agnes). Sees Sable and Aldric easily, other ghosts with effort.

**Partial sensitive.** Flickers and whispers, or exposure-induced (twins, Leo, Rook, Mara).

**Non-sensitive.** No perception; relies on charms or known tells (Chen, most people).

**True Seer.** A bloodline ability to see the future, sometimes skipping generations (the twins). Visions show danger and always come true. Seers were wiped out roughly a century ago.

## Places

**Ashfen.** Used for the crypt (ASH-17, ASH-18 designation) and as the series title. Whether it is the city's name is open.

**Alegrove Island / Alegrove House.** An isolated island estate. Site of the 1934 mass murder and the repeating loop.
- Aliases: the ruins (the modern derelict), 1934 (the restored loop house)
- Source: Alegrove arc

**The void (Cookie's).** Behind Cookie's mouth: a liminal space, a quiet wood under too many stars, centered on a small wooden cabin where the doll-maker lives.
- Source: Holy Rot arc

**The chapterhouse.** The Dawnchurch chapterhouse of the Origin arc. Struck from the records and closed a month after the ritual. The Hunger was sealed beneath it.
- Source: Origin arc
