# Vocab

Naming disambiguations the source material specifically calls out — not a full glossary, just the places where two things could be confused for each other.

**Daniel Vega vs. Daniel (Cora's partner).** The vampire-turned-hunter/covert operative introduced in `arcs/the-julian-mission.md` is **Daniel Vega** — the surname was chosen specifically to avoid collision with Cora's murdered business partner, **Daniel**, from `arcs/cora-winstead-the-frame-up.md`. Same first name, unrelated people, distinct surnames by design.

**Liza vs. Elena Dormanescu.** The frightened stealth-type vampire in Valeria's coven (`arcs/the-vampire-coven-valeria.md`) is **Liza** — distinct from the twins' mother, **Elena Dormanescu** (`characters/amelie-and-victor.md`).

**Mort Voss vs. Martin Dormanescu.** The mill consortium leader fighting the crypt's preservation order in `arcs/the-ashfen-crypt.md` is **Mort Voss** — distinct from the twins' father, **Martin Dormanescu**.

**Horatio Martinez, not Horatio Bates.** The memory-thief watchmaker in `arcs/horatio-martinez.md` is **Horatio Martinez** — "Mr. Martinez." Earlier drafts using the surname Bates are wrong and superseded.

**"Adam."** The name Aldric goes by with the Fischer family in `archive/the-fischer-family-the-fair.md` and `archive/the-pestilence-the-lamb.md` (both shelved, no longer part of the active story) — inferred from Greta's request to "contact Adam" rather than stated outright as a chosen alias anywhere else.

**The study / the library.** In `arcs/alegrove-island-murders/`, these are the same room; both names are used in-fiction interchangeably. See `arcs/alegrove-island-murders/loop-mechanics.md`.

**Voss.** Two unrelated characters share this surname: **Mort Voss** (above) and the drill sergeant **Voss** who trains the alternate-timeline Captain in `arcs/lichs-wheel/the-captain.md`. No relationship between them is implied anywhere in the source material.
