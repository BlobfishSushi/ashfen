# The Twins — Introduction

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [Cora Winstead — The Frame-Up](cora-winstead-the-frame-up.md).*

The vision that brings Amelie and Victor to the bakery.

## Position and dependencies

Seventh in fixed foundation order.

## What happened

The twins' worst vision yet: the entire school dropping dead simultaneously, themselves included. In the vision-death, they woke as ghosts — Amelie blind, Victor deaf, in that state — and found Sable and Aldric guiding the dead at the school. Sable offered to help them move on or come to the bakery.

They woke in the present carrying the vision's memory. Amelie painted the ghosts; Victor transcribed talk of "a bakery." They tracked the bakery down and convinced Leo their visions were real, since Amelie had painted Sable and Aldric without ever meeting them.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Amelie and Victor join the standing cast from here — see `characters/amelie-and-victor.md`. Whether their visions are premonitions or something else isn't settled until the next arc.
