# Museum Theft Aftermath

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [Leo Castello — Introduction](leo-castello-introduction.md).*

The short, quiet fallout from stealing the banishment bell.

## Position and dependencies

Fourth in fixed foundation order. Directly follows the heist in Leo's introduction arc.

## What happened

The bell has stayed hidden at the bakery ever since; Leo carries a replica in the field (see `world/objects.md`). The bell's disappearance became a famous unsolved heist — the thief seemingly bypassed all security with foreknowledge of guard routes. Investigation yielded nothing. The bell never resurfaced publicly.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

No new complications; this arc exists to close out the heist's public-facing side. The real bell stays at the bakery going forward, available to the crew but never carried into the field — that's what the replica is for.
