# Hans Fischer / The Piano

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: the emitter (see [Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter](yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md)).*

Aldric meets a living descendant of the aunt he never knew he had — and doesn't yet know it.

## Position and dependencies

Requires the emitter, since Hans sees Sable and Aldric solidified. Was originally a dependency for The Fischer Family / The Fair, which is now shelved (see `archive/index.md`) — this arc stands on its own in the active story. Full background on the lineage this arc touches is in `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md`.

## What happened

Hans Fischer, 23, a music student from the northern hills, walks into the bakery for the sourdough — he looks exactly like Aldric: curls, jaw, build. Aldric recognizes him as a Fischer-line descendant, connected through Aldric's aunt Aura rather than through Aldric's own mother's line, which ended with Aldric. Hans has no idea any of this history exists.

Hans has been practicing on a haunted conservatory piano. A spectral wire starts wrapping his throat, draining him; the ghosts remove it, but it returns. Investigation: the piano holds the residual impression of David Laskin, a student who hanged himself with piano wire sixty years ago. The physical wire is still in the mechanism and resists removal — the pattern defends itself.

Sable and Aldric solidify to extract it together, the wire fighting back around their throats (they don't need to breathe, so it doesn't matter). They burn it in the alley. Hans sees the wires around their necks, the chanting, the blue flame — he doesn't believe in ghosts, and accepts "researchers doing piano maintenance" as an explanation. He and Aldric bond over the northern hills, sheep, and an old dialect Aldric still knows, dead a century in the living world. Hans assumes he's found a distant cousin from a lost branch of the family.

## Characters introduced

- **Hans Fischer** — 23, music student, northern hills. Descends from Aura, which makes him Aldric's cousin many generations removed rather than a descendant. In the shelved `archive/the-pestilence-the-lamb.md` material he'd eventually be told the family history directly; as canon stands, he never is. Doesn't recur elsewhere in the active story.
- **David Laskin** — Conservatory student who hanged himself with piano wire sixty years before this arc. His residual impression, not a full ghost, haunts the piano Hans practices on and animates the wire. Resolved (burned) by the end of this arc; doesn't recur.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Aldric has a living relative for the first time since his own death, though he doesn't know the fuller history — see `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md` for what that history is. The Fischer Family / The Fair and The Pestilence / The Lamb, which would have carried this thread forward and eventually corrected Hans's belief that Aldric is a distant cousin, are both shelved (see `archive/index.md`) and no longer part of the active story. As canon currently stands, that correction never happens: Hans keeps believing he's found a lookalike cousin from a lost branch of the family, and Aldric never learns of Aura, Lamby, or the entity that killed his own family.
