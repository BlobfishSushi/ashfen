# The Marcus Webb Case

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [The Twins — Introduction](the-twins-introduction.md).*

The case that reveals what the twins' visions actually are.

## Position and dependencies

Eighth in fixed foundation order.

## What happened

Marcus Webb, a groundskeeper and former hunter, plotted to steal the school's life force for himself. The twins had visions of Marcus catching and killing them mid-investigation, letting them avoid that fate — revealing that visions reflect events that *did* happen in another timeline, sent back for a chance to prevent them.

The bakery crew and hunter network took Marcus down together. Rook identified the twins afterward as true Seers.

## Characters introduced

- **Marcus Webb** — Groundskeeper and former hunter. Plotted to steal the school's life force. Taken down by the combined crew and hunter network; doesn't recur past this arc.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Confirms the mechanic behind the twins' visions (see `characters/amelie-and-victor.md`) and confirms them as true Seers rather than an unclassified sensitivity — a distinction that matters because Seers were wiped out roughly a century ago as too dangerous to leave alive. See `world/hunter-network.md` for how the network classifies threats and allies.
