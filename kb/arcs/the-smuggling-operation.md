# The Smuggling Operation

*Tag: `[CORE · PLACEHOLDER]`. Depends on: — (no dependency on other post-foundation arcs).*

A stub arc: the twins get pulled into a case involving their own father's employer, and the case forks the timeline depending on what their parents learn.

## Position and dependencies

Any point after the foundation arcs. The source material for this arc is itself a stub — kept short here to match.

## What happened

The twins get pulled into an investigation of a transportation company suspected of smuggling supernatural goods. Their father, Martin Dormanescu, works for the company, entirely oblivious to all of it.

## Characters introduced

- **Martin Dormanescu** — The twins' father. Works for the smuggling company at the center of this arc, oblivious to what it's actually moving. Unaware of the twins' abilities by default.
- **Elena Dormanescu** — The twins' mother, a fighter pilot. Unaware of the twins' abilities by default. Her role only activates in the branch where she finds out — see below.

## Branch point

This case has a potential outcome where the twins' parents discover their abilities.

- **(A) Parents don't find out.** Status quo continues. If the entire bakery crew is later wiped out, this is the branch that opens onto a bad end — filed separately as an alternate ending rather than a normal listed consequence, since it requires erasing the whole standing cast first. See `archive/alternate-endings/the-hydra.md`.
- **(B) Parents find out.** Was explored in The Mother's Framework / Training, and its downstream arc Ezekiel & Cookie's Nature — both have since been shelved and are no longer part of the active story. See `archive/index.md`.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Nothing further is written for this arc as of this pass. Branch (B) and its downstream material are shelved; branch (A) remains open, with the Hydra alternate ending sitting off to the side of it rather than as its normal continuation.
