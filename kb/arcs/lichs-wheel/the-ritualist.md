# The Ritualist

The alternate Aldric: same person, same fever-orphan origin, raised at a militant fortress rather than an archive, in a timeline where the Hunger was never released. Dead as of this arc's end, in his own world, but his story doesn't stop at his death. See `overview.md` for the arc that produces him and `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for the home-timeline Aldric.

## Origin and training

Sole survivor of the same northern-hills fever that killed home-timeline Aldric's family, brought south at twelve by clerics performing rites across the dying region, the same as his counterpart. The fortress trained him as a ritualist rather than a fighter: he reads spirit signatures and wields a set of tuned bells that can stagger, disrupt, or, on undead, confirm or deny coherence.

Met the captain at fourteen (she was twelve), gave her the name "Sable" at sixteen, and stayed partnered with her for twelve years afterward, knowing the mortality odds, because she's the only person who understands what having nothing else left is like. He herds, she executes.

## The notebook

Keeps a blood-stained field notebook of witness testimony from lich attacks. It's this research that connects the bakery's wheel to the lich the fortress is already hunting, working out that it's one half of a matched escape-hatch pair and that a second, completed wheel exists somewhere in the mountains. See `overview.md` for the hunt that follows.

## Death

During the final fight on the lich's platform, the lich identified him as the fight's linchpin, pinned in place holding the field and guarded only by the captain's speed. The lich baited an opening by charging Leo and Agnes at the runework junction, drawing the ritualist's attention away just long enough for a small thrall to land a necrotic injection directly into him. The poison spread fast, muscle first, then organs. He kept ringing the bells from a seated position against the wall as his legs failed, holding the field as long as his hand would answer, until the bells finally stopped.

## The epilogue

The necrotic injection killed him, but its energy also anchored a fragment of him as a masterless thrall. He woke alone on the platform three days later: no lich, no captain, no crew, all of it resolved before he came to. He confirmed the captain's death by looking over the platform's edge, her body beside the lich's bones in the void, and understood the wheel's completion meant the crew had gone home.

Newly undead, his bells now read his own signature as "close undead," and the necrotic mark visibly marred him worse than his own ghost counterpart ever was. Running on finite borrowed animation, confirmed by his bells growing steadily quieter as the energy depleted, he needed no food, water, or rest, and set out on foot to find Aura, the aunt Aldric had told him about the morning before the final fight.

He worked backward from his own home village, now resettled by unrelated drifters after the fever, with no memory of "Aura" among them but a fading regional rumor of a reclusive "witch" the church had once unsuccessfully hunted, roughly twenty years prior. The trail grew colder outward through smaller settlements outside the village's immediate radius, until a farmer's wife recognized the name immediately: confirmed Aura's daughter Nettle, married two summers prior to a local man, Joel, pregnant, keeping a small quiet homestead up the stream with a notable flock of sheep, including one rumored never to age (Lamby, unrecognized by anyone as anything but local color).

Joel overheard the cleric's inquiry, recognized the danger from what Nettle had told him of her mother's history, and used a hidden shortcut to reach the homestead roughly an hour ahead of him. Aura, Nettle, and Joel prepared defensively: reinforcing wards, keeping Joel visibly present as an "ordinary shepherd family" cover story, folding Lamby into the flock, uncertain whether the visit meant discovery-and-flight or something else, since a single armed militant cleric matched neither a standard query nor a standard arrest profile.

Lamby and the wards detected his approach and identified him as undead before he arrived. The wards stopped him bodily at the property's edge, which he recognized immediately as unusually sophisticated, self-taught warding. He called out for Aura by name.

Aura came out to meet him, safe from him while inside her own ward-line, and he identified himself: Aldric, Marta's son. Aura, who had believed her entire birth family, Marta included, dead in the fever for twenty years, with no surviving knowledge that a son had survived, recognized her sister's curls and then her sister's face on him immediately. She broke down, embraced him, and held him as the last of his borrowed necrotic energy ran out. He died smiling, having completed the one thing he'd set out to do, with a last vision of the captain waiting for him, dryly noting a three-day search was slow, even for him.

Aura buried him near where the flock gathers at dusk, keeping his notebook and bells, his only possessions, as the sole proof he'd existed and found her at all. Nettle and Joel witnessed the aftermath without full context; Aura offered only that he was Marta's son, and that she knew nothing else of his life, death, or how he'd come to find her. The homestead returned to its routine, with one new grave among the sheep.

This does not touch present-day Aura's canon, in which she never learns her home-timeline nephew survived at all. See `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md` for her full history and `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for what home-timeline Aldric does and doesn't know about her.
