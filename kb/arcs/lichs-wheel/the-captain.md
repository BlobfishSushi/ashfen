# The Captain

The alternate Sable: same person, completely diverged, from a timeline where the Hunger was never released and the eastern chapterhouse grew into a militant fortress instead of an archive. Dead as of this arc's end, in her own world. See `overview.md` for the arc that produces her and `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for the home-timeline Sable.

## Origin

Caught at ten stealing bread from the fortress kitchen, same as home-timeline Sable was caught stealing from the chapterhouse kitchen. The difference is who found her: not a gentle archivist but **Voss**, a drill sergeant who saw a weapon instead of a child. She was trained young, in isolation, feared by her peers, and picked up the nickname "the mad dog."

## Personality

Efficient and violent, resigned to dying young: her own standing joke is "good odds so far" against surviving past twenty-six. Youngest captain in the fortress's history by twenty-four, with the highest mission success rate in the fortress and the highest squad turnover alongside it. Trusts only her ritualist.

## Relationship to the ritualist

Met a twelve-year-old Aldric at fourteen, the sole survivor of the same northern-hills fever that killed his family, brought south by clerics performing rites across the dying region. She gave him the name "Sable" at sixteen. Twelve years partnered since: he herds, she executes. She is the only person unafraid of her, and the only person she trusts with anything.

Rook and Mara privately read the captain/ritualist dynamic as their own hunter "cleansing-focus" burnout pattern: talented, doctrine-bound people who've stopped protecting anything of themselves and will very likely die young on an assignment nobody pulls them off of. See `overview.md`.

## Death

Died during the lich hunt (full sequence at `overview.md`), grappling the lich bodily and dragging him over the platform's edge into the void after he charged the worktable where the crew was completing the second wheel. Took a mortal wound to the side in the process; both fell together. Buried a week later by a church search team that never learned what happened in that chamber.

Sable and Aldric descended into the void afterward to say the old church rites over her body and the lich's remains, alongside the rites already said for the ritualist. See `the-ritualist.md`.
