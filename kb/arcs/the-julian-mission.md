# The Julian Mission / The Siren Vampire

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: the emitter (see [Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter](yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md)).*

A bait operation against a siren-type vampire that goes badly wrong for Sable and Aldric before it goes right.

## Position and dependencies

Requires the emitter to exist, since the plan depends on the ghosts operating fully solid. Is itself a dependency for [The Vampire Coven / Valeria](the-vampire-coven-valeria.md).

## What happened

A siren-type vampire, Julian, has been killing club-goers at Velvet (owned by vampire Celeste) — victims leave charmed and compliant, later found dead with no signs of foul play. A young hunter, Daniel Vega, goes missing investigating him before this arc's events.

The ghosts go in as bait using solid forms, aiming to draw Julian out and signal Rook and Mara, waiting outside. Julian targets Sable and Aldric — his charm works through voice, eyes, and touch, touch being worst. He kisses them in an alley to reinforce the charm and takes them to his Victorian house. Sable manages to hit the panic button when his attention splits toward Aldric.

**What Julian does to them.** He spends hours undressing and touching them, using the charm to make them want it. They forget their own names but hold onto each other throughout. He's fascinated by their bond and tries and fails to break it.

**The rescue.** Hunters arrive thirty minutes after the panic signal and find Julian standing over the still-charmed ghosts with their blood on his lips. Julian is captured.

## Characters introduced

- **Julian** — Siren-type vampire. Kills club-goers at Velvet through voice-, eye-, and touch-based charm. Captured at the end of this arc after assaulting Sable and Aldric.
- **Celeste** — Vampire; owns Velvet. Not directly involved in Julian's capture, but takes in Daniel Vega afterward and teaches him to feed safely. Recurs across the later vampire arcs as a stable, non-hostile presence.
- **Daniel Vega** — Young hunter, missing at the start of this arc after investigating Julian alone. Turned by Julian; hides in a cave for weeks, surviving on deer blood. Found by Rook and Mara with the ghosts' help. Celeste takes him in, teaches him to feed safely and control his siren pull. Officially dead in hunter records; unofficially still hunting. Continues as a major figure in [The Vampire Coven / Valeria](the-vampire-coven-valeria.md). *(No relation to Daniel, Cora's murdered business partner from her frame-up arc — the name overlap is coincidental.)*

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Technically a success — Julian captured, Daniel Vega recovered — and it doesn't feel like one. Sable and Aldric don't use the emitter for months afterward, flinch at sudden sounds, and stay withdrawn; Agnes and Leo keep vigil. See `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for how this sits alongside their other vulnerabilities. Daniel Vega's turned, off-the-books status carries forward directly into The Vampire Coven / Valeria.
