# The Bakery / Agnes

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [Origin](origin.md).*

How Sable and Aldric ended up haunting a bakery, and how Agnes became their front to the living world.

## Position and dependencies

Second in fixed foundation order. Requires Sable and Aldric to already be ghosts (Origin).

## What happened

Sable and Aldric were drawn to the bakery because its bread recipe resembled something from their own era. Agnes was present when Rook — hunting them — shot the ghosts with salt. She was already mildly sensitive; the blast unlocked full clarity. Injured, the ghosts fled to a warehouse. Agnes tracked them down and offered bread instead of fear.

Rook traced her there. Agnes talked him down: he agreed to let the ghosts go and call off future hunts on them. Agnes invited the ghosts to haunt the bakery. Over time, solving problems together, she became their front and bridge to the living world.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

This arc sets Agnes's standing role for everything after it: she's the crew's bridge to the living, the clearest perceiver of Sable and Aldric, and the one who brokered the peace that lets Rook work with the ghosts instead of against them. See `characters/agnes.md` for the role in full and `world/perception-tiers.md` for how her clarity compares to everyone else's. Rook's side of the same peace is in `characters/rook-and-mara.md`.

The salt-round injury is also the first confirmed vulnerability on record for Sable and Aldric — see `world/ghost-mechanics.md`.
