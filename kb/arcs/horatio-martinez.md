# Horatio Martinez / The Memory-Thief Watchmaker

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: — (no dependency on other post-foundation arcs).*

An eldritch watchmaker takes all four hundred years of Sable's memories overnight, convinced he's helping her. Unresolved as written — this is the most open-ended arc on the board.

## Position and dependencies

Any point after the foundation arcs. No dependency.

## What happened

**What he is.** An eldritch being presenting as a kindly old watchmaker, running a small watch and clock shop. Nothing about the presentation is a disguise he drops; it's simply what he is when he's dealing with people.

**What he does.** He reads an entire life trajectory in a single glance — everything that happened, everything it's leading toward. He then calculates which memories, removed, would improve that trajectory, and builds a bespoke watch for the person that takes them. He isn't stealing for gain. He's correcting, by his own accounting, and he's never wrong by that accounting.

**The vault.** Removed memories are kept, not destroyed — stored through a door in the back room of the shop, in a space with no ceiling.

**Sable.** He reads her and builds her a watch of a complexity nothing else in the shop matches — four hundred years of accumulation to account for. It takes her memories overnight. All of them.

**The morning after.** Sable wakes as a happy, unburdened twenty-six-year-old woman who doesn't recognize anyone in the room, Aldric included. She isn't distressed. That's the horror of it: nothing has been taken from her that she can feel the absence of, and by every measure Martinez uses, she's better off.

**On his ethics.** He's a utilitarian, and a coherent one. Consent arguments don't move him — not because he dismisses them, but because they don't register as an input to the calculation he's running. He isn't written as a villain, and the arc doesn't resolve him into one.

## Characters introduced

- **Horatio Martinez** — Mr. Martinez, a kindly old watchmaker; in fact an eldritch being that reads life trajectories and edits them by removing memories. Utilitarian, courteous, never wrong by his own accounting. *(Naming note carried from the source: he is Horatio Martinez, not Horatio Bates — earlier drafts using the other surname are wrong.)*

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Sable chooses to reclaim her memories, knowing what she's choosing to take back on. The group prepares to return to the shop with Sable leading.
