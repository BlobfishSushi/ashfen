# The CT Scans

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [The Ashfen Crypt](the-ashfen-crypt.md).*

Routine scientific study of Sable and Aldric's own bones turns into a recurring ordeal.

## Position and dependencies

Requires the bones already excavated and in the university's collection — see The Ashfen Crypt. Is itself a dependency for [Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter](yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md).

## What happened

Kaia's team CT-scans the remains. The X-rays pull Sable and Aldric into their own bones — a doubled awareness at first. Technician Derek runs repeat scans troubleshooting what looks like corrupted data; each scan reinforces the bond. By the eighth scan the ghosts are trapped in their bones for over a week, unable to move or speak except to each other. Agnes and Leo check on them repeatedly. They're freed when the bond fades.

## Characters introduced

- **Derek** — CT lab technician. Runs the repeat scans that trap the ghosts, chasing what he thinks is a data-corruption problem. Terrible taste in music. Recurs only as background lab presence in later arcs; never learns the full truth himself.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Ongoing: every semester a new grad student scans the bones, and the ghosts get pulled in every time. They've learned to endure it and listen in on the students — it's become a minor university legend. The bones stay in the collection as "scientifically significant." The corrupted scan data from this arc is the direct trigger for [Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter](yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md).
