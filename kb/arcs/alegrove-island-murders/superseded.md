# Superseded Mechanics

Retired mechanics from older drafts of this arc, kept so earlier material can still be read against something. None of the following is current; see `overview.md`, `loop-mechanics.md`, `loop-progression.md`, and `resolution.md` for what replaced each.

- **Three anchor guests (Banker, Journalist, Priest) and a ten-person roster.** Cut. They were load-bearing when the loop had a rotating killer and are dead weight under the placeholder mechanic.
- **Rotating fugue-state killer.** Retired in favour of the placeholder (see `loop-mechanics.md`).
- **Memory recovery by finding your own body.** Retired; the dead wake whole in the ruins instead.
- **The water exception (hidden dock and cliff bodies).** Retired.
- **Corruption of the ruins on the detective's death.** Retired.
- **The pantry sequence** (Sable locked up, gassed, escaping with a hairpin). Retired with the reordering to the current seven-guest sequence, under which Rook dies before Sable can be blamed for anything.
- **Physician's suicide by cliff jump.** Replaced by the tincture taken in the library, matching the false death.
- **The layers merging in the flood.** Considered and cut. The ruins and 1934 stay separate through the flood; see `loop-mechanics.md`.
