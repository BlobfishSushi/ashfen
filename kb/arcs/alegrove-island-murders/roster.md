## Reading the invitation-name column

Each guest's invitation carries a name in a smaller hand beneath the hidden text (see `loop-mechanics.md` for how the text is revealed). That name is not another guest. It is the person *that guest killed* before ever setting foot on the launch, printed on their own card and meaningless to anyone else who reads it. Seven guests, seven prior killings, none of them Alegrove's: the massacre punishes people who had already gotten away with murder once.

## Two connections worth noticing

**Aldric's card reads PAUL WINSLEY.** Winsley is the abusive father Sondheim smothered years before the massacre, and therefore Elaine Winsley's father. The man Aldric is implanted to have killed is the same man the letter-writer's whole life pivots on.

**Leo's card reads THOMAS ARDRY.** Ardry is the man driven to suicide by the original Detective's tunnel-vision investigation, the grievance the entire massacre is built on. The identity Leo is cast as carries, unknowingly, the wound that Sondheim spent the whole plot avenging.
