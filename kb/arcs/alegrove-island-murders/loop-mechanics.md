# Loop Mechanics

## Casting and sequence

Casting is fixed, per `roster.md`, and so is the sequence: same people, same order, same methods, same places, every cycle. There is no whodunit local to the current loop. What varies is only what the crew works out and when.

## Implanted biographies

Each counterfeit carries the genuine 1930s life of the person they play, real but cut off at the boat: a full childhood, relationships, a career, opinions, nothing about Alegrove. Aldric holds nineteen years of Liam Sondheim's medicine, a partnership that didn't suit, a sister called Margaret in Kettering, and a smothered man in 1919, and nothing of what Sondheim did on the island. He carries the architect's entire motive as lived memory without knowing it is a motive.

This is why the loop is not a puzzle box to be picked apart from outside: the guests are not lying to each other or performing ignorance. They believe themselves to be Ray Graves, Vivian Abernathy, Liam Sondheim, and so on, completely, until the moment the loop ends.

## Win and reset conditions

**Win.** The truth of 1934 (what was done, to whom, by whose hand) declared aloud by a guest, heard by all seven living guests at once. Only possible before the first death, since after that the number of living guests who can hear it starts dropping.

**Reset.** All seven dead, followed by the flood (below).

## The placeholder

The killings in every cycle are carried out by an invisible, man-height, man-shaped entity filled in with nothing: not a person, not the Physician, not a ghost, not Yates. There is no Yates; the will-reading is cover story, not fact.

- **It can be spoken to.** It calls itself a placeholder and accepts "Mr. Yates" as a convenience. Its stock answer to any question about motive or identity: *I am doing what was done. Who did it is the question. That part is yours.*
- **Manner.** Courteous, unhurried, without malice. Apologises for inconvenience.
- **It cannot be eliminated.** Leo can murder every other guest and the deaths continue on schedule around him regardless.
- **It improvises but is bound by the record.** Every death must land on its assigned person, method, and place. It will spend four minutes herding a fleeing woman uphill into the trees rather than shoot her in the open, because the record says snare, not gunshot.
- **It uses what is already on the island**: the snare, the rifle in the tool shed, the blowpipe in the bathroom closet, a pistol out of a dead woman's coat pocket.
- **Two deaths are not its work.** The Detective's shooting (by the Actress) and the Physician's suicide were guest acts in 1934, and the loop reproduces them by reproducing the circumstances that produced them, not by puppeting the killers. Sable genuinely shoots Leo, every loop, sincerely convinced of his guilt.

## The invitation

Every guest arrives with one: the house's own document, the terms of its exit, put into seven pockets.

**The house's position.** It runs the punishment because that is what it is. It cannot solve itself; the mechanism only terminates on a correct declaration spoken by a guest. So it supplies the terms and withholds the answer.

**Surface text (printed).** An invitation to Alegrove House for the reading of the late Mr. J. Yates's will, Friday the 12th of October 1934, launch departing Harrow Point at four.

**Hidden text (brought up by heat, drying the card near a fire):**

> You were invited because of what you did. There are seven in this house and not one of you clean. Yours is written below. We know the name. Ask the others and see what they say.
>
> This house does not end. It has not ended since 1934 and it will not end while what happened here goes untold. Say what was truly done, and to whom, and by whose hand, say it so that all seven hear it, and the door opens. Nothing less has ever worked. A great many have tried less.
>
> There are a great many dead in this house, and they will bring you to your final destination.
>
> Death is not the end of it here. The house will not let you go so easily as that. Use the time you are given.

Beneath, alone, in a smaller hand: the name of the person that guest killed (see `roster.md`).

**Deliberate ambiguity.** The line about the dead bringing you to your final destination reads as a threat. It is not; it describes where the record is kept.

**Reveal method.** Leo discovers the hidden text by accident in loop one, when his card comes through the storm soaked and he holds it to the drawing room fire to dry it. The text does not survive the reset as a physical object: the card must be re-dried and the personalised names re-harvested every loop.

## The two layers

Two distinct spaces, named for what they are rather than for which one is "real": the **ruins** and **1934**.

### The ruins

The modern-day derelict mansion the crew actually arrived at. Crew members killed in 1934 wake here as themselves, with both sets of memories intact: their own life, the implanted pre-island biography, everything they did in the role, and their own death.

- **No memory between loops.** Whatever the dead work out here dies with the cycle.
- **Nothing can be touched.** They pass through everything. It is a reasoning problem, not a searching one.
- **Arrival order is ragged** and does not track death order; some come up long after others who died later.
- **The seven bodies are laid out here** by the fixed record (right person, right method, right place) regardless of what actually happened this cycle. This is the crew's primary clue and their primary confusion, since the bodies can contradict what they just watched happen.
- **No sun, no wind, no gulls, no shift in light, no way to keep time.** The sea moves and the air does not.

### 1934

The restored house the loop runs inside. Sable and Aldric, when killed, persist here rather than crossing to the ruins, as ghosts perceptible only to Leo (see the ghost anomaly, below), believing themselves to be Vivian Abernathy and Liam Sondheim with no knowledge of their true selves.

## The flood

At the end of every cycle, once all seven are dead, a storm arrives out of nothing and the water rises across the whole island, not as waves but as a level coming up everywhere at once. It fills the ruins and 1934 alike. It does not drown the dead; it brings a slow grey cold that puts them out like a light, and then the loop resets.

**The layers do not merge.** Those in the ruins drown together; Sable and Aldric drown alone in 1934. Neither group can see the other.

**The illusion breaks in the last minutes.** As the flood comes, Sable and Aldric recover their real identities and see 1934 for the scenery it is, with only minutes left. They remember the loop just ended but not any before it.

## The ghost anomaly, in full

The house's two worst casting choices: it cast the performer and the healer without accounting for either being dead already.

- They die on schedule in 1934 and never reach the ruins.
- **Only Leo can perceive them.** To everyone else he is a man talking to a corridor, in a house where people are dying and he has already been suspected.
- They do not persist the reset.
- **In an uninterfered loop Leo never meets them**, because both die after he does.

### The anomaly that cracks the case

Across loops one and two, Aldric dies in the study on schedule and appears nowhere: not in 1934, not in the ruins. Sable also never appears, but she dies last and Leo is never alive afterward to check.

In loop three Leo kills both by his own hand, out of position: Aldric by gas in the boot room, Sable correctly by knife at the boathouse, changing only the hand. Both come up as ghosts.

This rules out method, order, room, and hand, leaving only *what they are.* Which should mean Aldric appears in loops one and two too, and he doesn't.

That contradiction is the thread. The resolution, reached in loop four: in loops one and two Aldric did not die. The study death is the Physician's false one (see `overview.md` for the truth of 1934). Nobody comes back from a death that didn't happen.
