# Resolution

Loop five's declaration (see `loop-progression.md`) ends the cycle. The house collapses.

## Where everyone wakes

Everyone wakes on the real island, unharmed, in the position their body occupied at the end of the loop rather than the position they died in during 1934: Agnes in a bed on the staff landing, Rook at the top of the cliff rather than below it, Sable a ghost on the clean dock, Aldric in the library, Cora on a path with no snare in the trees, Mara in the drawing room with bare plaster behind her, Leo in the boathouse.

It is Sunday. There are gulls. The ferryman arrives on schedule and finds five odd tourists and no massacre to report.

## What the others keep

Only Leo retains the full five loops. The others hold a fading impression of the fifth loop only: being someone, a man walking in out of the rain and saying a great many things that turned out to be true, a shadow coming apart, and a plea about a child.

## Elaine Winsley

Found in hospice care and told the truth: that Liam Sondheim killed her father, killed six people on that island, and killed himself last, and that the one thing he took back, at the end, was her. She takes it in, then chooses to keep remembering the kind doctor rather than the murderer, and says so plainly.

## Leo's notebook

Leo writes the whole case up in a small black notebook, the sort a country doctor carried, including the four murders in his own hand from loop three, and files it. He does not tell the others. See `overview.md` open threads for whether that changes.
