# Leo's Loop Progression

Only Leo carries memory between cycles (see `overview.md`). Five loops, each spent, learned from, and lost to the next reset except for what he keeps in his own head.

## Loop 1: blind

Watches all six of his people die believing it permanent. Discovers the invitation's hidden text at the fire (see `loop-mechanics.md`). Breaks down the cook's door looking for answers and makes himself the prime suspect in the process. Is shot in the boathouse by Sable, who has built a sound case against him from a stone room's worth of thinking. Wakes in the ruins with the others; the flood takes them.

**What he learns:** the shape of the sequence, the hidden text exists, and that acting like a detective inside the loop gets him killed on schedule anyway.

## Loop 2: prevention

Everything spent on keeping people alive. Everyone dies anyway, including one death he causes himself while trying to prevent another.

**What he learns:** the sequence cannot be broken by care. Effort redirected at saving people doesn't bend the record; it just changes who is holding the knife.

## Loop 3: the experiments

Kills four of his own people to isolate the ghost variable: Aldric (gas, boot room, early, his hand), Rook (poison, hall, early, his hand, the living control), Cora (snare, correct place and method, his hand only), Sable (correct in every particular, his hand only). Liam's ghost accompanies him for the whole of it.

**What he learns:** it is not the hand, the method, the order, or the room that decides who becomes a ghost. It is what Sable and Aldric are.

## Loop 4: the doctor

Focus turns entirely to Aldric. Establishes that no poison enters him by any route Leo can observe, that the smell at his death is sweet and floral and matches nothing in the medical bag, and that the cook's poison and the doctor's are different substances. Asks Liam, hypothetically, what kills sweet and fast, and is told about datura-and-monkshood tinctures and the old stories of suspended animation. Waits for the study death, then puts a scalpel into the body, and the ghost stands up.

**What he learns:** the Physician's death is false. The Physician is the architect.

## Loop 5: the declaration

Gathers all seven in the drawing room within the first hour and says the whole thing aloud: the order, the methods, the fake death, the motive, and the Physician's name. The placeholder walks in and the shadow falls away to reveal Sondheim. He does not regret the six; he regrets Elaine. He asks Leo to tell her.

This loop breaks the cycle. See `resolution.md` for what happens next.
