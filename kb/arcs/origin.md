# Origin — Sable & Aldric

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: — (first foundation arc).*

How Sable and Aldric died, and how they became what they are now. Everything else in their present-day life sits on top of this.

## Position and dependencies

First arc in fixed foundation order. Nothing precedes it.

## What happened

Prior Maren, a senior cleric at the Dawnchurch chapterhouse, accidentally released the Hunger — an ancient entity sealed beneath the building — and poisoned Sable to keep her from exposing it. Sable died believing it was fever. What Maren was actually covering was two facts at once: the release itself, and that the chapterhouse's passing rites had been corrupted by the Hunger, so every soul given those rites — including Sable's own, performed by Maren over her body — was being delivered to it instead of wherever they were meant to go.

**Days 0–3.** On the other side, Sable works out that the afterlife is wrong, the rites are wrong, and that she herself had performed those same corrupted rites over other people's dead for years. She crawls back out on that fury. Three days. Aldric is the first person she finds.

**Days 3–13.** Sable, Aldric, Tem, and head archivist Pell uncover the conspiracy and prepare a ritual to seal the Hunger back behind its door. The ritual needs a sacrifice; Sable, already dead, is the intended one.

**Day 13.** Maren sabotages the ritual, aiming to kill everyone who uncovered the conspiracy — Pell, Tem, Aldric — and pass the whole thing off as an accident. The sabotage invalidates Sable as the sacrifice: the door won't take her, and won't close. Aldric gives himself in her place as a last-minute patch and dies at the door at 24. Sable is pulled through with him regardless. Pell and Tem survive; Maren's plan fails against everyone it targeted and succeeds only against the two people it wasn't aimed at.

Sable and Aldric spend twenty years trapped together in the Hunger's dimension before Tem gets them out — the foundation of the bond they carry into the present day. See `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for who they are now and how that bond shows up day to day.

## Aftermath — the chapterhouse's collapse

The church buried it. A prior sabotaging a sealing ritual and directly causing two deaths was not a scandal it chose to survive, so the chapterhouse was struck from the records and closed about a month later. Its clerics were scattered, deliberately kept apart.

## Characters introduced

- **Maren** — Prior at the chapterhouse. Released the Hunger by accident, then chose cover-up over confession, which cost her nothing until it cost two clerics their lives. Exiled afterward to somewhere cold and quiet; no trial, no charge, nothing written down. Lives out the rest of her life there.
- **Pell** — Head archivist. Took Sable in off the streets at ten and later took Aldric in as an orphan of the fever (see `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md`). Helps uncover the conspiracy and prepare the sealing ritual. Sent to an archive in the south after the closure; dies there of old age.
- **Tem** — Fellow cleric, part of the conspiracy-uncovering group. Goes into hiding rather than take a new posting and spends twenty years researching a way to pull Sable and Aldric out of the Hunger's dimension — discovery would have meant being locked up. Succeeds at the end of that twenty years.

Pell and Tem manage very little correspondence after the split, but both spend the rest of their lives trying to preserve some record of what actually happened.
