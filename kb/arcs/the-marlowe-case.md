# The Marlowe Case / The Haunted Apartment

*Tag: `[CORE]` — brings in Rook and Mara. Depends on: [Museum Theft Aftermath](museum-theft-aftermath.md).*

A haunted apartment building that the crew can't solve alone, and the case that turns the hunters into allies.

**What's actually load-bearing here:** most of this case — the haunting itself, Marlowe's ghost, the entity — isn't what makes the arc foundation-fixed. The one beat that is: during the investigation, Leo gets himself discovered by the hunter network, which sends Agnes and Leo to the hunters' bar, brings Mara into the story, and through her, Rook. That's the load-bearing thread; everything else below is the case that gets them there.

## Position and dependencies

Fifth in fixed foundation order. At this point the working crew is just Leo, Agnes, and the ghosts — Cora, the twins, and the wider circle haven't joined yet. Happens some time after Leo became a bakery fixture.

## What happened

The building's superintendent, Marlowe, gets caught up in a haunting and is killed by the entity in the process. His ghost tracks down the bakery for help. The case turns out to be beyond what the crew can solve on their own.

Taking a leap, Agnes and Leo go to the hunters' bar to reach out to Rook, who'd left himself as a contact back when he called off the hunt on Sable and Aldric after the salt shot (see [The Bakery / Agnes](the-bakery-agnes.md)). This is where Mara is first introduced: at the bar, she catches sight of the banishment bell replica Leo has hidden under his jacket.

The hunters help remove the entity from the apartment; Marlowe is able to move on. In the process, Leo effectively becomes a hunter under Mara's jurisdiction — some tension follows, but once Rook and Mara learn about the ghosts and choose to cover for them, they become permanent allies.

## Characters introduced

- **Marlowe** — Apartment superintendent, killed by the entity haunting his building. His ghost is the one who brings the case to the bakery; moves on once the entity is removed. Doesn't recur past this arc.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Rook and Mara join the standing cast from here on — see `characters/rook-and-mara.md`. Leo's status as an unofficial hunter under Mara's jurisdiction is set here and referenced throughout later arcs. See `world/hunter-network.md` for how the network works and why covering for the ghosts is controversial inside it.
