# Cookie's True Nature

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [The Marcus Webb Case](the-marcus-webb-case.md).*

How Cookie joined the bakery, and the first (partial) look at what she actually is.

## Position and dependencies

Ninth in fixed foundation order. Note: this arc introduces Cookie and the crew's first look at her nature, but it is not the arc that fully explains her — see the note on [The Holy Rot / Cookie's Void](the-holy-rot-cookies-void.md), much later, which is the canonical reveal.

## What happened

Mr. Patterson, who'd taken in Cookie ten years prior — an injured kitten on an ordinary patch of grass, for reasons she has never disclosed to anyone — died peacefully of old age but didn't move on, unwilling to let Cookie end up in a shelter. His ghost, with Cookie following, reached the bakery. He asked Agnes to care for Cookie and find her a home; Agnes agreed.

That night, a hungry Cookie revealed her true nature as an eldritch entity that had spent ten years disguised as a cat, hunting Sable and Aldric as prey through the streets and winning. Mr. Patterson's ghost caught her and gave her a stern talking-to: no eating people. She hasn't chased them since. He is the first person Cookie ever bonded with, and she keeps the rule out of love for him — the only constraint on her, voluntary, and not transferable to anyone else.

## Characters introduced

- **Mr. Patterson** — Took Cookie in as a kitten ten years before this arc. Dies of old age; his ghost's request is what brings Cookie into Agnes's care and gets her to stop hunting Sable and Aldric. Moves on at the end of this arc; doesn't recur.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Cookie joins the standing cast, in Agnes's custody, from here on — see `characters/cookie.md`. The "no eating people" rule established here holds for the rest of the timeline, and is the reason the crew is later blindsided by what she's actually capable of.
