# The Vampire Coven / Valeria

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [The Julian Mission](the-julian-mission.md).*

Vampires assembling under a figurehead, a real power planning a war on the hunter network, and Daniel Vega's first major operation as a covert asset.

## Position and dependencies

Requires Daniel Vega already turned and recovered — see The Julian Mission.

## What happened

Vampires assemble in the city under a compulsion-type figurehead, Kira. Grouping is rare and worrying. Daniel Vega goes undercover as a siren seeking community, is accepted, and earns trust among Liza (a frightened stealth type), Greg (turned against his will), and Varick (a scarred combat type and former soldier, who clocks Daniel Vega as an ex-hunter but doesn't expose him).

Kira turns out to be a figurehead; the real power is Valeria, an ancient stealth type who has spent decades building influence, planning coordinated strikes on the hunter network followed by open vampire rule. She recruits Daniel Vega, seeing predator potential in him.

**Captured hunters.** Kai, Jess, and Benny are caught scouting the warehouse. Daniel Vega bites all three to keep his cover, then partially lifts the charm to explain the plan; they play along. He keeps them fed and alive, arguing against killing them.

**Resolution.** Daniel Vega works with Rook and Mara: neutralizes a hunter safe house without casualties via a staged extraction, and cultivates doubt among wavering vampires. When Valeria accelerates toward war, he uses his charm at full strength to incapacitate her in her own war room. Rook and Mara take down the combat types; Varick extracts non-combatants. War prevented.

## Characters introduced

- **Kira** — Compulsion-type vampire; figurehead of the citywide gathering, fronting for Valeria without controlling anything herself.
- **Valeria** — Ancient stealth-type vampire; the real power behind Kira. Spent decades building influence toward a coordinated war on the hunter network followed by open vampire rule. Incapacitated by Daniel Vega's charm and taken down at the arc's climax.
- **Liza** — Young stealth-type vampire in Valeria's coven; frightened, not a willing participant. Distinct from the twins' mother, Elena Dormanescu.
- **Greg** — Vampire, turned against his will; part of Valeria's coven.
- **Varick** — Scarred combat-type vampire and former soldier. Clocks Daniel Vega as an ex-hunter and keeps it to himself. Joins Daniel Vega as a fellow covert operative after the arc.
- **Kai, Jess, and Benny** — Three young hunters captured scouting Valeria's warehouse. Kept alive under partial charm by Daniel Vega and played along with his cover. Survive the arc knowing he's alive, and keep the secret.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Daniel Vega stays officially dead and becomes an established covert operative for Rook and Mara; Varick joins him. Kai, Jess, and Benny know he's alive and keep the secret. Celeste takes in the non-combatant vampires from Valeria's coven — see `arcs/the-julian-mission.md` for who Celeste is.
