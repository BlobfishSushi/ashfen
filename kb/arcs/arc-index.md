## Key dependencies

**The emitter is the hinge.** Six arcs need it. In a timeline without it, they do not happen as written. Note that three of those six (Julian, Echo State, Fischer/Piano) are marked key even though the Emitter arc that unlocks them isn't.

**Alegrove** sits anywhere after the crew is assembled. It needs Leo, Agnes, Sable, Aldric, Rook, Mara, and Cora all present, and no emitter. What carries out of it: Leo holds five loops of memory nobody else has, and a written record he does not share.
