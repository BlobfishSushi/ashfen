# The Echo State / The Spider

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: the emitter (see [Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter](yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md)).*

A hunting-support mission that spirals into Sable and Aldric reliving their own deaths on loop, then into a pocket reality that almost keeps them there.

## Position and dependencies

Requires the emitter to exist. This version supersedes any earlier draft of this arc.

## What happened

The ghosts scout for Rook and Mara, tracking a giant spider taking hikers. It ambushes the hunters, webbing them toward its nest. The ghosts solidify, shield Rook and Mara with their own bodies, and take multiple spikes of chitin and silk through chest, side, thigh, shoulder while dragging them clear. Massive blood loss — spirit energy — drains them into echo states: reliving their deaths on loop, before they collapse.

Rook and Mara recognize the state from experience; most spirits never come out of it. Back at the bakery, Stephanie drops the solid charge to stabilize them. For five days the crew watches the echoes: Sable dying of fever at 26, asking Aldric to stay, unaware of Maren's poisoning; Aldric in the ritual chamber at 24, discovering the sabotage and walking through the door in Sable's place. Full context on both deaths is in [Origin](origin.md).

**The crossing.** Around loop 500, Sable senses a thread to the *other* echo — Aldric's, not her own. She starts deviating from the script; the loop stutters. The two begin reaching for each other across their separate deaths. Sable burns through centuries of accumulated energy to cross into Aldric's echo, appears beside him at the door, and begs him to let the world end instead. He stays. The echo — with no script for an Aldric who refuses to sacrifice himself — shatters, releasing a massive energy surge that visibly staggers the crew and blows out Stephanie's equipment.

**The pocket reality.** The crossing doesn't just release energy — Aldric carries residue absorbed from the Hunger's dimension during his original twenty years trapped there, dormant until now. Combined with Sable's catalytic burn and four centuries of accumulated spiritual mass, it crystallizes the impossible ending they just made real (ritual fails, Hunger released, world ends) into a pocket dimension. The crew is pulled in bodily — intangible, invisible ghosts in a looping four-minute recreation of the chapterhouse on the day of the original ritual, reliving it from the outside as witnesses who can't intervene, growing gradually more tangible each loop.

**The resolution.** By loop seven the crew is tangible enough for Rook to reach Aldric directly. The only fix is completing the ritual as it was always meant to end — door closed, sacrifice made — except now the door has become indiscriminate, willing to take one or two. Sable refuses to let Aldric go alone; the cost is true oblivion, not the twenty-year exile of the real timeline. She agrees instantly. They fall together; Pell and Tem, present in the loop, grieve a version of the sacrifice that never happened in the real timeline. The pocket reality collapses; the crew returns to the bakery, tangible, in the same moment they left it.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Sable and Aldric wake with no memory of the echo state or the pocket reality — the last thing either recalls is the spider. The crew agrees, unspoken, never to tell them. Rook and Mara come away with a strong, unprovable instinct that whatever makes the ghosts different from other spirits is tied to their twenty years inside the Hunger's dimension — no precedent exists to confirm it, and they can't say what it means, only that the ghosts' ceiling for danger and power is higher than the network's threat categories account for. See `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` and `characters/rook-and-mara.md` for how this lands on each of them going forward, and `world/ghost-mechanics.md` for echo states as a general phenomenon.
