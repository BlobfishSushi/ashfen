# Cora Winstead — The Frame-Up

*Tag: `[CORE]` — brings in Cora and Chen. Depends on: [The Marlowe Case](the-marlowe-case.md).*

A murder frame-up that brings Cora into the crew and gives Leo his one contact on the force who knows.

## Position and dependencies

Sixth in fixed foundation order.

## What happened

Cora's business partner Daniel was murdered by his mentee Peter after Daniel discovered Peter had been embezzling from the firm. Daniel's ghost warned Cora and named Peter as the frame-up's architect. Cora was arrested three days later. A lawyer friend connected her to Leo.

In the interview cell, Leo brought Sable and Aldric. Cora's attempt to fake not seeing them failed, and she came clean about being a medium. Leo took the case. Over one week, the ghosts monitored Peter, gathered evidence, and protected witnesses.

**Chen's discovery.** During the investigation, a car chase after Peter let Chen — a detective on the force and Leo's friend — see Leo impossibly know Peter's route: Sable was chasing the car, Aldric shouting directions from above. Chen worked out something supernatural was going on; Leo confirmed he works with ghosts, without giving full details. Chen became a trusted contact — the only person both aware of the supernatural and on the force.

Peter was arrested; Cora was freed.

## Characters introduced

- **Daniel** — Cora's business partner and best friend of fifteen years, co-founder of their architecture firm. Murdered by Peter after discovering the embezzlement; his ghost's warning is what starts the arc. Doesn't recur past this arc. *(No relation to Daniel Vega, introduced later in the vampire arcs — the name overlap is coincidental.)*
- **Peter** — Daniel's mentee. Embezzled from the firm, murdered Daniel to cover it, and framed Cora for it. Arrested at the arc's end. Doesn't recur.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Cora and Chen both join the standing cast from here — see `characters/cora-winstead.md` and `characters/chen.md`. This is also the first time Chen learns the supernatural is real; his ongoing role as Leo's inside contact on the force is set here.
