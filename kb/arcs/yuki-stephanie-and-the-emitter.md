# Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [The CT Scans](the-ct-scans.md).*

The arc that invents the emitter — the single biggest hinge in the post-foundation timeline. See `arcs/index.md` for the full list of arcs that require it.

## Position and dependencies

Requires the corrupted CT scan data to already exist (The CT Scans). Everything downstream that needs the emitter — Julian Mission, Echo State/Spider, Hans Fischer/The Piano, The Fair, The Vampire Coven, and (via The Fair) The Pestilence — depends on this arc having happened.

## What happened

Yuki Tanaka, a grad student in Kaia's lab, works out the truth fast and confronts Kaia directly. She joins the circle, helping protect the ghosts during scans.

Yuki sends the corrupted CT data to Stephanie Constable, a signal processing engineer at a defense contractor. Stephanie finds the interference is structured, not random — she builds a filter that decodes it into images of the ghosts, and finds a related frequency that *amplifies* spiritual energy instead of trapping it.

Stephanie builds the emitter: it makes the ghosts solid, tangible, visible. Tested first in the bakery, then in the city at night, then in daylight. Solid-form tells: dilated pupils, cold skin, no pulse, voluntary breathing that sometimes falls into unnatural rhythms. Dogs sense something's wrong; cats don't care.

**The trials.** Durability tests — pain response, blood loss, injury recovery. Blood turns out to be spirit energy in liquid form; massive blood loss means energy depletion. An amplification frequency makes the ghosts fully solid and interactive for a few hours at a time. First use: they eat bread, hold hands, read books. Food falls through them when the charge fades.

## Characters introduced

- **Yuki Tanaka** — Grad student in Kaia's lab. Works out the truth independently and confronts Kaia about it, then joins the circle protecting Sable and Aldric during scans. Continues as a background presence in the university-adjacent arcs.
- **Stephanie Constable** — Signal processing engineer at a defense contractor, Yuki's friend. Decodes the CT interference and builds the emitter; runs the trials. The emitter's ongoing custodian and operator across every arc that uses it.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

The emitter itself, and how it works mechanically day to day, is documented as a standing object in `world/objects.md`. This page keeps the origin story — how it came to exist and who built it. From here forward, any arc tagged as depending on "the emitter" assumes this arc has already happened.
