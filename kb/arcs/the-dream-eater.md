# The Dream Eater

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [Cookie's True Nature](cookies-true-nature.md).*

The illusion that shifts Sable and Aldric's relationship for good.

## Position and dependencies

Tenth and last arc in fixed foundation order. Everything after this point can be sequenced freely relative to the other post-foundation arcs.

## What happened

The crew was trapped in a sophisticated illusion, engineered by an entity — the Dream Eater — that feeds on sustained, believed-in dreams rather than on fear or life force directly. Inside it, Sable and Aldric built a life together and fell in love. Rook disrupted the illusion on their engagement night, before it went further.

## Characters introduced

- **The Dream Eater** — The entity behind the illusion. Feeds on dreams sustained long enough to feel real to the dreamer. Its full nature, motive, and fate past this arc are unwritten; it doesn't recur elsewhere in the log as written so far.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Shifts Sable and Aldric's relationship from purely platonic to something deeper, though reality pulled them back from where the illusion had taken them. See `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for how this sits alongside their four centuries of platonic partnership. This is the last foundation arc — everything from here forward assumes the full early cast (Sable, Aldric, Agnes, Leo, Rook, Mara, Cora, Chen, the twins, Cookie) is already in place.
