# Leo Castello — Introduction

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: [The Bakery / Agnes](the-bakery-agnes.md).*

How Leo found the bakery, and how a museum heist made him a fixture there.

## Position and dependencies

Third in fixed foundation order. Requires the bakery already established as a place ghosts can be reached at.

## What happened

Leo, having heard rumors the bakery could help solve hard problems, approached Agnes about a cold case he'd left the force over. It turned out to be supernatural and still active. Agnes revealed the ghosts to him. Stopping the entity behind the case required stealing the banishment bell from a museum — see `world/objects.md` for what the bell is and does. They pulled off the heist together, dealt with the entity, and solved the case.

Leo became a bakery fixture afterward. Frequent use of a replica bell — made necessary by his growing caseload — knocked something loose in him: partial sensitivity to Sable and Aldric.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Leo's standing role — private investigator, partial sensitive, replica bell in the field — is set here. See `characters/leo-castello.md`. The heist itself has its own consequences, covered separately in [Museum Theft Aftermath](museum-theft-aftermath.md).
