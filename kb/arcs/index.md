# Arc Index

Every active arc in the Ashfen Chronicles, in two blocks. **Foundation** arcs are fixed order — each one assumes the arcs before it have happened. **Everything after** is order-undecided between arcs; pick per timeline, but the beats *within* an arc are sequential as written on its own page.

Unless tagged otherwise, an arc is `[CORE]` — happened, fixed, assume it in any timeline. See the tag legend below the tables.

⭐ marks a **key arc** — pure curation, the author's own pick of the arcs that shine the most light on the characters and drive the plot forward the most. It changes nothing about canonicity; it's a "read this first" flag, not a claim about what's true. See `conventions.md`.

Shelved arcs and alternate endings no longer appear in these tables at all — they live in `archive/`, out of the active tree. See the sections below the tables for what's there and why. Ideas that aren't arc-ready yet — still being explored, or too small to even be a draft — live in `work/brainstorm/` and `work/brainstorm/seeds.md` instead; nothing there is in these tables either, since nothing there has been decided yet.

## Shelved arcs

Five arcs were drafted, then deliberately pulled from the active story rather than continued. They're fully written and kept for reference, just no longer assumed in any current timeline. Full list, and why each one's there, at `archive/index.md`:

- The Mother's Framework / Training
- Ezekiel & Cookie's Nature
- The Pestilence / The Lamb
- The Fischer Family / The Fair
- The Morrison Building / The Serpent

Shelving these two removed the only remaining downstream use of Hans Fischer / The Piano's Fischer-family thread and of the Aura Chronicle's forward connection — both of those pages now say plainly that the payoff isn't coming, at least for now.

## Alternate endings

Two endings exist that break continuity with anything written after them, kept because they're interesting on their own terms rather than because they're going to happen. Full detail at `archive/index.md`:

- **The Hydra** — a bad end for the twins, reachable only if the entire bakery crew is wiped out first. Diverges from The Smuggling Operation, branch (A).
- **The Holy Rot / Cookie's Void — alternate ending** — Cookie's remaking goes further than a cure: Sable and Aldric come back as ordinary cats, permanently, with Agnes left to reckon with it. Diverges from the canonical Holy Rot arc above.

## The emitter is the hinge

Four active arcs — Julian Mission, Echo State/Spider, Hans Fischer/The Piano, and the Vampire Coven — require the emitter to exist. In a timeline that skips Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter, none of these four happen as written. (Two more arcs that also needed the emitter, The Fischer Family/The Fair and The Pestilence/The Lamb, are now shelved — see above.) Alegrove and the Wishing House are a different case: in both, the crew doesn't bring or use the emitter — a choice driven by what the job looked like going in, not a requirement that the emitter not yet exist. Either arc can sit anywhere after the crew is assembled, including well after Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter, once there's an in-story reason the team wouldn't have it on hand for that particular job. See the note below.

## Special positioning: Alegrove and the Wishing House

Both arcs slot in anywhere after the full crew is assembled. Neither uses the emitter — but that's the crew's call on the day, not a constraint on when the arc can happen. Nothing stops either one from being set after Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter exists in continuity, as long as there's a reason the team went in without it (didn't expect to need it, didn't have it charged, the job didn't read as supernatural enough to prep for):

- **Alegrove** needs Leo, Agnes, Sable, Aldric, Rook, Mara, and Cora all present.
- **The Wishing House** needs Leo, Cora, Rook, Mara, Sable, and Aldric — and additionally needs **Agnes absent**. The arc runs on the living being unable to perceive the ghosts at all; Agnes is the one bridge that would break that premise, so she can't be in the party.

## A note on arc 9's placement

Cookie's True Nature sits in the foundation list because it's early and load-bearing for the cast, but it is not the arc that finishes the reveal. The Holy Rot / Cookie's Void, much later, is the canonical account of what Cookie actually is; it supersedes the (now-shelved) Ezekiel placeholder's claim to that reveal. See `archive/ezekiel-and-cookies-nature.md` for the demotion note.
