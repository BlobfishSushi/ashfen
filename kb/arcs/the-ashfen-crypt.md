# The Ashfen Crypt

*Tag: `[CORE]`. Depends on: — (post-foundation, order undecided relative to other arcs in this block).*

A construction crew's discovery uncovers Sable and Aldric's own remains, and puts their history on a modern archaeologist's desk.

## Position and dependencies

Any point after the foundation arcs. No dependency on other post-foundation arcs, but it is itself a dependency for [The CT Scans](the-ct-scans.md).

## What happened

A construction crew breaks into an underground crypt holding two dozen bodies. Dr. Kaia Okonkwo (Cordova State University) takes over the site. ASH-17 and ASH-18 — the last interred, a woman and man buried side by side — are Sable and Aldric's remains. The crypt also holds a small iron-bound door, nailed shut, that won't open.

Leo and Agnes pose as genealogical researchers, feeding Kaia documents — letters from a woman named Edlyn, a traveling bishop's complaints, property records — to build a preservation case. Mort Voss, leading the mill consortium that wants the land, fights the order. A hearing grants six months' protection. The door stays sealed. Kaia eventually learns the truth about the ghosts. The crypt becomes a protected site.

## Characters introduced

- **Dr. Kaia Okonkwo** — Archaeologist at Cordova State University. Takes over the crypt site, is fed documents by Leo and Agnes to build the preservation case, and eventually learns the truth about Sable and Aldric. Recurs in [The CT Scans](the-ct-scans.md) and [Yuki, Stephanie, and the Emitter](yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md) as the lab's lead.
- **Mort Voss** — Leader of the mill consortium seeking to develop the crypt's land. Fights the preservation order and loses. Distinct from the twins' father, Martin Dormanescu. Doesn't recur past this arc.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

The crypt is protected, the bones stay in the university's collection, and Kaia knows the truth — all three conditions the next arc, The CT Scans, depends on. The small sealed door is never opened or explained in the source material; flagged as an open thread.
