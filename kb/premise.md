# Premise

Present day. A bakery in the city, run by a woman who can see the dead, staffed after hours by two ghosts who have been dead four hundred years and are still working. Around them: a private investigator, a small and territorial network of hunters, a medium, a pair of teenage seers, and a cat that is not a cat.

Cases come to the bakery because the bakery has a reputation. Most of them are hauntings, entities, or people. The register is horror by absence rather than spectacle: the thing that should be there and isn't.
