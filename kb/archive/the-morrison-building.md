## What happened

Cora alerts the crew to a medium convention in a building she condemned years ago, haunted by an ancient shadow-serpent that's fed on spirits for decades. The crew infiltrates. Everett Cross, the convention organizer, is a sensitive manipulated by the entity posing as his mentor. The entity is invisible to hunters — only mediums and ghosts perceive it.

Sable is pulled into a séance and bluffs the entity into retreating, claiming the crew is more powerful than they are. Everett is devastated to learn he's been used. Maya Ivanovna, a genuine medium who came hoping to switch off her abilities, joins the circle instead.

## Characters introduced

- **Everett Cross** — Sensitive; organized the medium convention. Manipulated for years by the serpent posing as his mentor. Devastated by the reveal; doesn't formally join the circle, but is left aware of the truth.
- **Maya Ivanovna** — Genuine medium. Came to the convention hoping to find a way to switch her abilities off; joins the circle instead.
- **The serpent** — An ancient shadow-serpent entity, invisible to hunters, that has fed on spirits at the Morrison Building for decades. Bluffed into retreating by Sable rather than defeated outright; its current whereabouts are unresolved.

Nothing — shelved on its own, with no other page depending on it. As written, Maya Ivanovna would have become an ongoing ally, and the serpent itself would have been driven off rather than destroyed, with its whereabouts unresolved.
