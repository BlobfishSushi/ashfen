# The Hydra

*Tag: `[ALT-ENDING]`. Diverges from [The Smuggling Operation](../../arcs/the-smuggling-operation.md), branch (A) — parents don't find out — and is reachable only if the entire bakery crew is wiped out. See `arcs/the-smuggling-operation.md`.*

A bad-end branch, not a normal continuation of branch (A): what happens to the twins if there's no one left to stop their kidnapping. Filed here rather than as a regular listed consequence of the Smuggling Operation, since it requires the story's entire standing cast to be gone first.

## What it requires

Continues from the conclusion of The Smuggling Operation, branch (A). **Only reachable if the bakery crew is wiped out** — Sable, Aldric, Agnes, Leo, Rook, Mara, Cora, Chen, and Cookie are all implicitly gone or unable to intervene. Without their absence, there's no one to stop the kidnapping described below, and the ending doesn't trigger.

## What happens

The head of the smuggling ring learns about the twins' Seer abilities and wants to kidnap them, incorporating them into the criminal network. With the crew gone, nothing stops it: the twins are held by the Hydra syndicate until age 24, becoming some of the most feared and wanted criminals associated with it. They eventually escape and return home to whatever friends and family are left, but their past keeps catching up as they try to build a normal life.

## Why this is an alternate ending, not canon

It requires erasing the entire supporting cast to happen at all, which makes it fundamentally incompatible with the ongoing story rather than a branch that could sit alongside it. Treat it as a distinct, self-contained bad-end timeline rather than something that folds back into the main line. See `characters/amelie-and-victor.md` for who the twins are before this branch diverges from them.
