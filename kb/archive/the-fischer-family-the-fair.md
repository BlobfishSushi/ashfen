**Shelved.** Not part of the active story — see `index.md`. Hans Fischer / The Piano remains active canon on its own; this is simply where the thread stops now, rather than continuing here.

## Position and dependencies

Required the connection between Hans and Aldric already established in `arcs/hans-fischer-the-piano.md`.

## What happened

Hans invites Aldric and Sable to the state fair — a de facto Fischer reunion disguised as a sheep showcase. They go using the emitter, in modern clothes, tells manageable in the chaos.

The family absorbs them easily: Hans's mother insists they're too thin; his father wants to talk sheep; his brothers share the hair. Greta (91), the family elder, holds Aldric's hand, notices the cold, and files it away without comment.

**The blessing.** Greta performs a family ward — salt, holy water, iron shavings — that would hurt the ghosts. She sees their panic, and when Hans intervenes, lets them skip it. She knows something's different; she doesn't press. Greta has practiced folk wards her whole life, some of which genuinely work — she recognizes Aldric isn't alive but also isn't dangerous, and files him under "family, regardless of nature."

## Characters introduced

- **Greta** — 91, Fischer family elder. Practices folk wards, some of which genuinely work. Recognizes that Aldric isn't alive and isn't dangerous, and keeps his secret without ever being told the details. Would have been central to [The Pestilence / The Lamb](the-pestilence-the-lamb.md), which is shelved alongside this arc.

Hans's parents and brothers appear in this arc but aren't individually named or developed past their roles at the reunion.

## Aftermath / what carries forward

Nothing, in the active story. As written, Greta's quiet acceptance of Aldric would have set up her request for him by name — "Adam" — in the next arc, when the family needed help.
