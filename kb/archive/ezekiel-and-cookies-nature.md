## What happened

Ezekiel, a werewolf-like boy, stumbles onto the bakery and is temporarily taken in by Agnes. During an uncontrolled transformation, Cookie eats him.

## Characters introduced

- **Ezekiel** — A werewolf-like boy. Wanders into the bakery and is briefly taken in by Agnes before an uncontrolled transformation gets him swallowed by Cookie. His fate afterward is unwritten.

Nothing — shelved along with its parent branch.
