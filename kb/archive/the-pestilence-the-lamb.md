## What happened

Months after the fair, a fever hits the Fischer farm — sheep first, then people. Vets and doctors can't identify or treat it. Greta falls ill and asks Hans to contact "Adam" (the name Aldric goes by among the Fischers) specifically, saying the lamb is hurt and the fever is getting through.

**The reveal.** The Fischers have been protected for some 400 years by a nature deity in lamb form — known to Aura alone as "Lamby," though no living Fischer knows that name — bound to the family by Aura, Aldric's aunt. An ancient pestilence entity in the hills periodically surges; the deity usually pushes it back, but this time it caught the deity off guard and wounded it badly, hence the fever bleeding through. This is the same fever, and the same entity, that killed Aldric's own immediate family generations ago — a fact Aldric has never known, having always believed it was an ordinary illness. Full detail on Aura, Lamby, and the entity's history is in `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md`.

Aldric tells Greta the full truth: many generations removed, dead centuries, a ghost. Greta takes it in stride and asks him to help the lamb. The pestilence is sealed in a cave by a working tied to Aura's bloodline, so only her bloodline can open the barrier — Hans opens it. The deity leads them there. Aldric and Sable drop the emitter and phase through the rock with the deity, leaving Hans alone in the clearing with a pile of empty clothes.
