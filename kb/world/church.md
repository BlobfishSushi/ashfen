# The Church, Then and Now

## The Dawnchurch

Ran chapterhouses with at least two branches that barely interacted:

- **Archival/support** (Sable, Aldric, Pell): researchers, archivists, support staff.
- **Militant** (frontline, bell-trained, crossed-torch-and-bell insignia): the fighting branch. Retired roughly forty years after Sable and Aldric's deaths.

Every cleric was bell-trained regardless of branch. If the chapterhouse came under attack it was all hands, archivists included.

## The Origin chapterhouse

The chapterhouse of the Origin arc was struck from the records and closed a month after the ritual. Its clerics were scattered deliberately.

- **Maren** was exiled to somewhere cold and quiet and lived out the rest of her days there. No trial, no charge, nothing written down.
- **Pell** was sent to an archive in the south and died there of old age.
- **Tem** went into hiding rather than take a posting, and spent twenty years researching a way to get Sable and Aldric out.

## The Office of Lesser Rites

An Inquisition-style religious sect, called "the Censers" by the hunters who take them. Deploys the holy rot (a slow-acting holy energy poison) against Sable and Aldric. Taken down by the hunters and Leo during the week Cookie has the ghosts in her void.
