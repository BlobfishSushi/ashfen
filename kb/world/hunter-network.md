# The hunter network

What's left of institutional monster-hunting, after the church's militant branch stopped existing as an institution. See `world/church-history.md` for that collapse.

## Structure

Remnants of old guilds plus independents who never belonged to one. Works less like an order than like mercenary work — small and close to flat.

**Recruitment.** Young, and almost always someone who lost or nearly lost somebody to the supernatural and came looking for answers. Nobody is born into it.

**City-level structure.** A senior hunter oversees a city, assigning jobs and collecting intel. Mara holds this position in Baustin, the bakery's home city (see `world/setting.md`; Ashfen is a separate town four hours away, where the original chapterhouse sits — not the same place), and the roster under her is not large. She has no visibility into what hunters in the next city are doing unless they make contact. Clusters don't agree on doctrine, don't share everything, and are under no obligation to. See `characters/rook-and-mara.md`.

**The back lines.** The structural part, and the reason a network exists at all rather than a scatter of freelancers: archives, artifact containment, ritual studies, training. They hold the resources, move information between clusters, and get called in when a job turns difficult or strange.
