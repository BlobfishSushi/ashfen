# The Aura Chronicle

*[BACKGROUND]* — roughly 420 years before the present day. This is settled canon, not a present-day arc: it happened, it is finished, and none of it is available to be replayed or altered. It exists in the kb to give texture and detail to the arcs it underlies, and to record what present-day characters might eventually learn about it.

Aura is Aldric's aunt — his mother Marta's youngest sibling, born into a shepherding family in the same unnamed northern-hills village that later raises Aldric himself, a generation before he's born. She is the origin point of two things that matter in the present day: the deity known as Lamby, and the pestilence entity that killed Aldric's family and now threatens the Fischers.

## The shape of her life

Aura shows real sensitivity from early childhood — she sees minor nature spirits invisible to everyone else and can coax plants to unnatural growth. In a devout, insular village this reads first as an odd child's imagination, then as something the priest, Ansel, reports to the chapterhouse. Her oldest sister Marta and her six brothers form a standing pact to shield her, at real cost to themselves, for years. At sixteen, done hiding and unwilling to keep costing her siblings, she leaves before dawn.

Alone in the hills, she meets a being holding back something wrong and sick that surfaces near her camp — old, lamb-shaped, neither good nor evil. She names it Lamby, over its objections turned amusement, and learns real nature-magic from it over years. A hunter named Joran passes through her life for a season and leaves without a word the moment he sees what she is; she's already pregnant when he becomes untraceable. Marta, defying Aura's own request, rides out to be present for the birth. The daughter is named Nettle.

The entity Lamby has suppressed for longer than the village has existed gets loose — provoked by an unrelated working somewhere else entirely — and threatens to sweep the region. Lamby can ward Aura, Nettle, and their land specifically, but only through a permanent blood pact: it costs Aura her safety from the church for life, and costs Lamby its own shape, diminishing it permanently into an ordinary-looking lamb. She accepts. The wards hold. They do not, and cannot, extend to her birth family. The fever kills her parents' entire remaining household inside two weeks, along with most of the region — this is the same fever that orphans twelve-year-old Aldric and sends him to the chapterhouse. Aura rides out afterward to find her village empty, one mass grave too large to count, and spends the rest of her life believing her whole birth family died in it.

She's wrong about one part of that, and never finds out. Years later, a shockwave reaches her the same night the sealing ritual traps Sable and Aldric in the Hunger's dimension. Secondhand rumor — a closing chapterhouse, a dead young cleric named Aldric, curls like hers — tells her enough to know her nephew survived the fever and died anyway, in a ritual the church called an accident. She never learns he survived as a ghost rather than dying outright. She rides to the chapterhouse, can't get in, gets confirmation from frightened local spirits, and rides home in silence.

She lives to 86, raising grandchildren and great-grandchildren who carry her siblings' features without ever being told why a strange working sometimes just works. Lamby stays with the family line for four centuries, unremarked as anything but "the old lamb," present at every birth, wedding, and burial. Full detail on all of this — including her relationship with the church, which never resolved into a clean verdict — is in `cast.md`.

## How this connects forward

**The lineage correction, stated plainly:** every present-day Fischer, Hans included, descends from Aura and Nettle — not from Marta, and not from Aldric. Marta's line ended with Aldric when he died at the chapterhouse door. This corrects earlier notes that implied Hans descended directly from Aldric; he doesn't. He's Aldric's cousin many generations removed, via Aldric's aunt, not his heir.

**Lamby** is still bound to the Fischer land in the present day, still holding the pestilence back on a fragile balance, and still unrecognized by any living Fischer as anything but an old lamb. **The pestilence entity** is the same one that killed Aldric's family — later re-sealed into a cave barrier keyed to Aura's bloodline, which is why a Fischer descendant (Hans) is the one who can open it. See `arcs/hans-fischer-the-piano.md` for how Aldric and Hans first meet — that arc is active canon. The family-reunion and pestilence-reveal arcs that were originally meant to follow it and pay this history off — `archive/the-fischer-family-the-fair.md` and `archive/the-pestilence-the-lamb.md` — have since been shelved and are no longer part of the active story (see `archive/index.md`). As canon currently stands, Aldric never learns of Aura, Lamby, or the entity, and this background sits unconnected to the present day, kept here for reference and in case the thread is picked back up. For present-day Aldric and what he does and doesn't know about his own aunt, see `characters/sable-and-aldric.md`.

**Not part of this canon:** an alternate-timeline version of Aura appears in the Lich's Wheel arc, briefly reunited with an alternate, dying Aldric. That's a different timeline's Aura, reached by different history, and it doesn't touch this Aura's canon — in this one, she never learns her nephew survived the fever at all. See the Lich's Wheel kb page for that material; it isn't duplicated here.
