# Perception tiers

Who can see ghosts and other spirits, and how clearly, sorted into four tiers.

1. **Born mediums** — see all spirits easily. Cora Winstead is the only standing-cast example: innate spirit perception, present from birth, kept hidden until her own case forced it into the open. See `characters/cora-winstead.md`.

2. **Sensitives** — a triggering event sharpened their sight. Agnes is the example: sees Sable and Aldric easily, other ghosts only with effort. See `characters/agnes.md`. *[Open: whether Agnes's triggering event is the salt-round incident in The Bakery / Agnes arc or something that preceded it.]*

3. **Partial sensitives** — flickers and whispers, or perception unlocked by exposure rather than a single event. Two distinct paths into this tier appear in the standing cast:
   - **The twins** — supernatural ability in their own right (true Seers), which grants a baseline sensitivity as a side effect. See `characters/amelie-and-victor.md`.
   - **Leo** — exposure-induced, specifically through the resonance of the replica banishment bell, carried and rung case after case. See `characters/leo-castello.md` and `world/objects.md`.
   - **Rook and Mara** — exposure-induced through years in hunter work. See `characters/rook-and-mara.md`. *[Open: what specifically made them sensitive — the job in general, hunter training, or a particular incident.]*

   All partial sensitives see and hear Sable and Aldric directly, less clearly than Agnes does.

4. **Non-sensitives** — most people, including Chen. Rely on charms or known tells like cold spots rather than direct perception. See `characters/chen.md`.

## Where this matters mechanically

Perception tier determines who can be a witness, a target, or an obstacle in a given scene — a non-sensitive can walk through a room where a ghost is actively speaking and register nothing; a partial sensitive in the same room gets a flicker they may or may not trust. The Wishing House uses this tier system as a core mechanic: it subtracts perception weakest-first as the arc runs (partial sensitives first, then Cora), and the arc specifically requires Agnes to be absent because her tier would break the house's central condition. See `arcs/wishing-house/overview.md`.
