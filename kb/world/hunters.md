# The Hunter Network

Modern hunters are what remains after the Dawnchurch's militant branch stopped existing as an institution. Remnants of old guilds plus independents who never belonged to one. It works less like an order than like mercenary work.

## Recruitment

Young, and almost always someone who lost or nearly lost somebody to the supernatural and came looking for answers. Nobody is born into it.

## Structure

Small and close to flat. A senior hunter oversees a city (Mara has this one, and the roster is not large) assigning jobs and collecting intel. She has no visibility into what hunters in the next city are doing unless they make contact. Clusters do not agree on doctrine, do not share everything, and are under no obligation to.

## The back lines

The structural part, and the reason a network exists at all rather than a scatter of freelancers: archives, artifact containment, ritual studies, training. They hold the resources, move information between clusters, and get called in when a job turns difficult or strange.
