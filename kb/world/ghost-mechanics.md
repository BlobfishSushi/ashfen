# Ghost mechanics

How ghosts form, what they can do, and what can hurt them.

## Formation

Generally one of three triggers:

1. **Supernatural ability in life** — e.g. the twins, in the branch where their vision-deaths occur. See `characters/amelie-and-victor.md`.
2. **Strong unfinished business.**
3. **Strong associated emotion** — anger or sadness. The most common and most dangerous category, and the basis for the old doctrine that all ghosts are dangerous.

Sable reads cleanly as category 3. Aldric fits none of the three cleanly, and nobody in-world has ever pushed on that gap — see `characters/sable-and-aldric.md` for what actually holds them both coherent, which is not common knowledge even to them.

## Capabilities

**Baseline** (default state, no tech or magic involved): can't touch objects. With effort, a ghost can turn visible, chill the air, and interfere with lights and electronics. They pass through walls and can reach places the living can't.

*[Open: the fine grain of the baseline — how reading, page-turning, and the archive work Sable and Aldric demonstrably do actually function under a can't-touch-objects rule. Also open: range — they travel to the northern hills and through a time-gate without difficulty, so no tether has ever been established.]*

**Solid form** *(arc-conditional — requires the emitter, see `world/objects.md`)*: solid, tangible, able to interact with the physical world for a few hours per charge. This is a conditional exception layered on top of baseline, not a replacement for it. In any timeline without the emitter arc (`arcs/yuki-stephanie-and-the-emitter.md`), baseline is the only mode available.

## Crossing over

Sable and Aldric can help another ghost cross over. Not a technique, ritual, or working — no object involved, nothing that requires the emitter. It rests entirely on having been dead a long time themselves and knowing firsthand what a newly-dead person is going through: they calm the newcomer down, walk them through what happened to them, and let them know that if their unfinished business is resolved and they're willing, there's a door with a warm light on the other side they can choose to walk through, to wherever comes next.

Neither Sable nor Aldric knows what's actually behind that door. They can only offer the reassurance itself, not a guarantee. It's the closest thing left to their old work as clerics.

Presumed condition, per how this first plays out on the page: the newcomer's unfinished business has to actually be resolved first, or the door isn't a real option yet. First shown in `arcs/leo-castello-introduction.md`, with Geoff.

## What harms ghosts

**This list has never been stated as a rule anywhere in-world — it's assembled here from what's actually happened across arcs. Treat any single line as provisional until it's been independently confirmed a second time.**

- **Salt.** A salt round injured Sable and Aldric badly enough to force a retreat. First and clearest confirmation, from `arcs/the-bakery-agnes.md`.
- **Ward mixtures.** Salt, holy water, and iron shavings together — Greta's family ward in `archive/the-fischer-family-the-fair.md` (shelved, no longer part of the active story) would have hurt them if performed.
- **The true bell.** Dangerous to Sable and Aldric as well as to whatever it's aimed at, and it does not distinguish between the two. **A ring injures rather than destroys** — the ghost equivalent of being hit by a truck: badly hurt, laid out, a long recovery, and no use to anybody meanwhile. **Severity scales with proximity**: across a room is survivable and awful, standing beside it is considerably worse, and nobody has established what holding it would do. See `world/objects.md` for the object itself and the practical consequences of this for every scene it appears in.
- **Holy energy — "the rot."** Accumulation-based rather than acute: presents as fever, no known cure outside Cookie's intervention. See `arcs/the-holy-rot-cookies-void.md`.
- **Blood loss in solid form.** In solid form, blood is spirit energy made liquid; massive loss means energy depletion, which can drop a ghost into an echo state — reliving their death on loop — until the bond fades or a passive charge sustains them. See `arcs/the-echo-state-the-spider.md` for the fullest demonstration of this, including what happens when the echo states of two linked ghosts start to cross.
