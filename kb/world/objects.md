# Objects and Devices

## The banishment bell

An old technique of the Dawnchurch's militant branch. Requires iron of a specific design, tuned to a precise resonance, plus the skill to ring it correctly, a knowledge lost to time. Every cleric was trained on the bells regardless of branch. Sable and Aldric can both ring one correctly, if not to a ritualist's standard.

**The true bell.** The only one known to exist. Stolen from a museum during Leo's first case and hidden at the bakery ever since. It genuinely banishes. It is heavy, a two-handed, planted-feet proposition, not something anyone carries. It is dangerous to Sable and Aldric as well as to whatever it is aimed at. It comes out only when the crew means to banish something and the something warrants it.

**Leo's replica.** Unblessed iron, right shape, wrong resonance. Stuns and repels, banishes nothing. A tool for buying seconds.

**Practical consequence.** The only two people who know how to use the true bell are the two it is most dangerous to, and neither can lift it without the emitter. Everyone else on the crew works from their instruction rather than training.

## The emitter (arc-conditional)

Stephanie Constable's device. Makes ghosts solid, tangible, and visible for a few hours per charge. Six post-foundation arcs depend on it existing.

**Solid-form tells:** dilated pupils, cold skin, no pulse, breathing that is voluntary and sometimes falls into unnatural rhythm. Dogs know something is wrong; cats don't care.

**Blood.** In solid form, blood is spirit energy made liquid. Massive blood loss equals energy depletion and can trigger an echo state.

**The Trials:** durability tests during development. Pain response, blood loss, injury recovery. First use: they eat bread, hold hands, read books. Food falls through them when the charge fades.

## The Lich's Wheel

A cartwheel-sized wooden wheel, stone-mounted, bone-inlaid, symbols too old and too northern-hills for the network's archivists to read. A lich's escape hatch, a one-way gate for undead energy. Incomplete: the destination ring was bare, no target marked. A worn override clause near the hub: absent a marked destination, the wheel defaults to "the nearest safe alternative" for undead energy. Activated when Sable read the trigger phrase aloud while translating.

Now in the hunter network's vault (contact: Delacroix), logged inert, flagged never to be read aloud or translated aloud again.
