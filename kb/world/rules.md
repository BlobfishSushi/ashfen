# World Rules

## Perception tiers

1. **Born mediums** (Cora): see all spirits easily.
2. **Sensitives** (Agnes): a triggering event sharpened her sight. Sees Sable and Aldric easily, other ghosts with effort.
3. **Partial sensitives** (the twins, Leo, Rook, Mara): flickers and whispers, or exposure-induced. Leo via the bell's resonance, Rook and Mara via years in the work. They see and hear Sable and Aldric directly, less clearly than Agnes. [Open: what specifically made Rook and Mara sensitive.]
4. **Non-sensitives** (most people, Chen): rely on charms or known tells like cold spots.

## Ghosts: formation

Generally one of three triggers:

1. **Supernatural ability in life.** (E.g. the twins, in the branch where their vision-deaths occur.)
2. **Strong unfinished business.**
3. **Strong associated emotion** (anger or sadness). The most common and most dangerous category, and the basis for the old doctrine that all ghosts are dangerous.

**Sable and Aldric's category, with an asterisk.** Sable reads cleanly as category 3, strong emotion. Aldric fits none of the three categories, and nobody has ever pushed on it. The hunters, and Sable and Aldric themselves, default to category 3 and have never had reason to look closer. What actually holds them both coherent is the Hunger's own essence. (Not known in-world.)

## Ghosts: capabilities

**Baseline (no tech or magic):** cannot touch objects. With effort: turn visible, chill the air, interfere with lights and electronics. They pass through walls and can reach places the living cannot.

**Solid form (arc-conditional, requires the emitter):** solid, tangible, able to interact with the physical world for a few hours per charge. A conditional exception, not a replacement. In any timeline without the emitter arc, baseline is the only mode.

## Ghosts: what harms them

Assembled from arcs rather than ever stated as a rule. Check against the event log before treating any line as fixed.

- **Salt.** A salt round injured both of them badly enough to force a retreat (the Bakery/Agnes arc).
- **Ward mixtures.** Salt, holy water, and iron shavings (Greta's family ward would have hurt them).
- **The true bell.** Dangerous to them, not only to what it is aimed at.
- **Holy energy.** The rot: accumulation-based, presents as fever, no known cure (see Holy Rot arc).
- **Blood loss in solid form.** Blood is spirit energy made liquid; massive loss means energy depletion, and can drop them into an echo state (reliving their deaths on loop) until the bond fades or a passive charge sustains them.

## Seer lore

True Seers: a bloodline ability to see the future, sometimes skipping generations. Visions show danger and always come true. Warning people directly never worked, so the twins document instead (Amelie draws, Victor writes).

Seers were wiped out roughly a century ago for being too dangerous to warlords and anyone with plans worth protecting. The last confirmed sighting predates that. Discovery means recruitment or elimination, often both in that order.

Visions reflect events that did happen in another timeline, sent back for a chance to prevent them (established in the Marcus Webb case).
