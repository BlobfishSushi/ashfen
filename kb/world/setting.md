# Setting

Where and when the Ashfen Chronicles takes place, and what kind of world it is underneath the bakery and its cases.

## When

Modern day — the present-day year is **2026**. Technology is roughly at parity with our own world: cell phones, CT scanners, signal-processing engineers building things like the emitter. Nothing about the tech baseline is deliberately behind or ahead of ours; it's a contemporary setting first, supernatural second.

## A different Earth

This isn't our world with monsters added — it's a different Earth that happens to share some geography and culture with ours. Some existing places and cultures carry over recognizably (New York, France are both referenced as real, existing places). But the nations aren't the same: **America, as a country, does not exist in this world.** The bakery's home country is a distinct nation that occupied similar ground, with its own history — one that already had people like Sable and Aldric (clerics, chapterhouses, the Dawnchurch) as far back as the 1600s.

**Religion.** Sporadic rather than dominant. Small sects have risen and died off quickly throughout this world's history, rather than any single faith consolidating the way major world religions did in ours. The Dawnchurch — chapterhouses, archival and militant branches, the whole institution behind Sable and Aldric's original lives — is one such sect: real, regionally powerful in its day, but never the kind of unchallenged institution that persists for millennia. This is part of why its collapse after the Origin arc (see `arcs/origin.md`) could be handled quietly — it wasn't too big to disappear. See `world/church-history.md` for what's known about the Dawnchurch specifically.

**Eldritch entities.** The supernatural in this world isn't a hidden layer bolted onto ordinary history — it's shaped that history dramatically, from the shadows, for as long as there's been a history to shape. Cookie, Horatio Martinez, the Hunger, the pestilence entity Lamby suppresses — these aren't anomalies in an otherwise-mundane past; they're closer to a hidden current running underneath most of what actually happened, visible only in fragments to the people (like Sable and Aldric's chapterhouse, or the present-day hunter network) equipped to notice.

## Geography

The bakery sits in **Baustin**, a mid-sized city — closest in flavor to a mid-sized American city, though again, this isn't America.

**Ashfen** is not Baustin. It's a separate, small **town**, roughly a **four-hour drive** away, and it's the site of the original chapterhouse — the crypt where Sable and Aldric's remains (ASH-17 and ASH-18) were excavated four centuries later. See `arcs/the-ashfen-crypt.md` and `arcs/origin.md`. The series title and the crypt's name both come from that town, not from Baustin, where the present-day cast actually lives and works.

**The country is deliberately unnamed.** Not an open question — a settled choice. It's gone through war and enough name changes over its history that the name doesn't quite matter anymore; nobody in-world reaches for it as an identity the way they would a city or a neighborhood. Treat any future reference to "the country" as intentionally vague rather than a gap to fill.
