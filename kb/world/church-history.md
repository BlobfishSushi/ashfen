# The church, then and now

*See `world/setting.md` for how the Dawnchurch fits the wider religious landscape — small sects rising and dying off quickly, rather than one dominant faith.*

## The Dawnchurch

Ran chapterhouses, with at least two branches that barely interacted:

- **Archival/support** — Sable, Aldric, and Pell all belonged to this branch. Researchers and archivists, not frontline fighters. See `characters/sable-and-aldric.md`.
- **Militant** — frontline, bell-trained, crossed-torch-and-bell insignia. Retired as an institution roughly forty years after Sable and Aldric's deaths. This is the branch the present-day hunter network descends from; see `world/hunter-network.md`.

Every cleric, regardless of branch, was trained on the banishment bells — if a chapterhouse came under attack, it was all hands, archivists included. See `world/objects.md`.

**Correction on the branches' relationship, confirmed via `arcs/lichs-wheel/overview.md`:** the militant branch was active and contemporary during Sable and Aldric's own era. They simply belonged to the archival branch and stayed out of militant politics, which is why the two branches read, in the world/character material, as more separate than "active at the same time" might suggest.

## The chapterhouse's fall

The chapterhouse of `arcs/origin.md` was struck from the records and closed roughly a month after the sealing ritual; its clerics were scattered deliberately, on the church's institutional decision that a prior sabotaging a ritual and killing two young clerics was not a scandal worth surviving in public. See `arcs/origin.md` for the full account of what happened and to whom afterward (Maren, Pell, and Tem all have separate fates worth reading there).
