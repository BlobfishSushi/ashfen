# Seeds

Ideas too small to be their own page yet — a spark, an image, a "what if," a beat with no shape as a story yet. Not arcs, not drafts, just a running list so nothing gets lost while it's still this small.

When an idea grows enough to need real structure — characters, stakes, dependencies — give it its own page in `work/brainstorm/` instead (tag `[EXPLORING]`) and remove it from this list. See `conventions.md` for how that tag works and `work/brainstorm/index.md` for what happens after.

## Ideas

- A regular notices Agnes setting out cups of tea at the empty corner table — the one nobody ever touches, that always goes cold — and mistakes it for a grief ritual. Hands her a leaflet for a grief support group, tells her it's helped him a lot if it applies. Agnes doesn't know how to respond at first.
