## Lifecycle

A page here goes one of two places once a decision gets made:

- **Committed** → moves into `arcs/` (or `kb/world/`, `characters/`, etc., whichever fits) with a real canonicity tag (`[CORE]`, `[CORE · PLACEHOLDER]`, etc.), and comes out of `work/brainstorm/`.
- **Passed on** → moves into `archive/` with the `[SHELVED]` tag instead.

Either way, nothing here is meant to be permanent — it's a waiting room, not a destination.
