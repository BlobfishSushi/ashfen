# The House Before the Bakery

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`. Predates `arcs/the-bakery-agnes.md` — would sit before the current earliest point in Sable and Aldric's present-day timeline.*

## Premise

Before Sable and Aldric ever find the bakery, something on the edge of the city catches their attention — it "smells good," which is odd on its own, since smell isn't usually how they register anything. Chasing it leads them to a house crawling with genuinely hostile entities, kept fed by some kind of ritual running in the basement — a ritual that, as it turns out, is also sustaining Sable and Aldric themselves at the time. The entities use the ritual to lure the house's tenants down to the basement.

The tenants are stubborn college students who would rather blast music to drown out the creepy laughter, or tape a slamming cabinet shut, than actually move out — because the rent is unbelievably cheap.
