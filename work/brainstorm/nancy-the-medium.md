# Nancy

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`.*

## Premise

A four-year-old full medium, Nancy, comes into the bakery with her mother, who's completely oblivious. Nancy sees Sable and Aldric easily and asks them about their clothes. Aldric ends up teaching her to knit. Agnes, worried that a four-year-old has no way of knowing that not every ghost is a safe one, calls in Cora to help.

## Characters

- **Nancy** — four years old, a full medium. Sees ghosts as easily as Cora does, with none of the caution that comes from knowing what that means.
- **Nancy's mother** — unnamed so far. Doesn't know.

## Notes

Sits naturally next to Cora's own introduction as the crew's born medium (`characters/cora-winstead.md`) and the tier system on `world/perception-tiers.md` — Nancy would be the youngest sensitive on the board by a wide margin, and the first one the crew has to protect from her own gift rather than help come to terms with it.
