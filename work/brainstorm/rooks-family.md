# Rook's Family

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`. A side story built on family background `characters/rook-and-mara.md` and `arcs/wishing-house/rooms.md` already establish — a closer look at the family that's there, not a new one.*

## Premise

Rook's family: older brother **Ross** runs the mob; younger sister **Rosalind** is blind and paralyzed from the waist down, since the category-three incident that also killed their father. Rook became a hunter a few years afterward. Rosalind, more sensitive than Rook, can tell he's working with something supernatural; Ross has no sense of it at all. Rook and Ross keep a standing don't-ask-don't-tell about each other's respective sketchy business.

## Established and not up for revision

`characters/rook-and-mara.md` and `arcs/wishing-house/rooms.md` (Room 1) already fix the following:

- Surname **Manchetti**. Parents **Terry** and **Cathy**; birth order is Ross, then Rook, then Rosalind.
- The category-three incident happened when Rosalind was twelve and Rook was seventeen: their father was killed, Rosalind was blinded and crippled.
- Rosalind told the family something was in the hallway beforehand; seventeen-year-old Rook told her there was nothing there.
- Rosalind is faintly sensitive in her own right, and always has been.
- The Wishing House's Room 1 rebuilds the apartment as it was in 1998, with Rosalind fourteen, sighted, and walking — that's the illusion the house built, not this draft's timeline.

Any version of this draft has to land on those beats, not around them.

## New in this draft

- **Ross runs the mob.** Not previously specified what Ross does; this draft commits him to organized crime.
- **The don't-ask-don't-tell arrangement** between Ross and Rook, presumably dating from around when Rook became a hunter.
- **Timing:** Rook became a hunter a few years after the accident, rather than immediately.

## Open questions for this draft

- Whether Ross knows Rook is a hunter specifically, or just that Rook is "in something" he doesn't ask about — the don't-ask-don't-tell could cut either way.
- Whether Rosalind being "more sensitive than Rook" is a claim about degree (both are partial sensitives per `world/perception-tiers.md`; this would just rank them) or about kind.
- Whether this plays as a present-day story — a case that brushes against Ross's world, or against Rosalind — or another look backward, the way Pell's page (`pells-perspective.md`) looks backward at Sable and Aldric.
