# Mara's Family

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`. A side story built on family background `characters/rook-and-mara.md` and `arcs/wishing-house/rooms.md` already establish. The family dynamic itself is being held as more complicated than either account alone — see the note below — with the specifics deferred to drafting.*

## Premise

Mara's family: a total-recall prodigy with no magic aptitude at all, born into a witch family that shuns her for it in some ways and isn't cruel about it in others — a dynamic more complicated than either half alone, to be worked out on the page rather than decided in advance. She left to become a hunter, even though her own family sits closer to the kind of things the network hunts than most people ever get near.

## Established and not up for revision

`characters/rook-and-mara.md` and `arcs/wishing-house/rooms.md` (Room 2) already fix the following:

- Born **Devlet Manoli**, into eleven generations of working witches.
- Total recall for ritual structure; can hear a working done wrong from the next room.
- Tested for aptitude at three, four, six, nine, eleven, and thirteen — nothing, every time.
- Left the family at nineteen for the hunter network.
- Has protected the family since: eleven hunter enquiries deflected in eight years, two falsified site reports, and something under a stone lid in the yard she's never spoken of.

## The family dynamic — held open, not resolved

`characters/rook-and-mara.md` says plainly that **"nobody was cruel about it"** — the family's word for her lack of aptitude is closer to "an unlucky thing that happens to a household" than a judgment on her. This draft adds that the family also shuns her for it.

Author's call: both are true at once, in their own way — not a contradiction to pick a side on, but a real complexity in how this family treats her. What "shuns" looks like when nobody involved would call themselves cruel, and where that line actually sits scene to scene, gets worked out when this is drafted, not here.

## New in this draft

- **Her family sits closer to the things the network hunts** than most families do — consistent with the existing "the kind the network confiscates or burns," stated more directly here.
