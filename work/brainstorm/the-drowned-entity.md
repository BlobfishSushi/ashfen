# The Drowned Entity

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`.*

## Premise

Rook and Mara catch a case: something unknown is killing sailors and fishermen, hiding in the waterways and sewers, and only surfacing during storms. Cora gets pulled in because of a looking-glass device that can be tuned to pick up "eldritch entity" frequencies, loosely defined — its sensitivity scales with the sensitivity of whoever's holding it, which is why it needs Cora rather than anyone else on hand. Sable and Aldric help search the water itself and guide any confused victims still stuck down there.

Partway through the search, Cora's glass catches a strange signal pointing in an unexpected direction — toward Sable and Aldric. She notices, and lets it go for the moment. Once everyone regroups, she points the glass at them directly out of curiosity, and the signal is strong enough that she throws up on the docks. What she saw wasn't two ghosts — it was their essence, directly, and it's too strange and frightening to put into words.

## Characters

No new named characters yet — runs on Rook, Mara, Cora, Sable, and Aldric. The entity itself is unnamed and unidentified as of this draft.
