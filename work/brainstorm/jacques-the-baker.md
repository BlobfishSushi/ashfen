# Jacques

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`. Quieter and lower-stakes than the rest of this batch — a character beat rather than a case.*

## Premise

A second bakery opens in the neighborhood. Agnes's new-customer traffic drops — her regulars keep coming just as consistently, and the bakery is still plenty to cover the bills, so she's mostly fine with it; if anything, she'd been running a little too busy before. What she doesn't expect is for the other bakery's owner to come by and introduce himself.

The other baker, Jacques, is a Frenchman who moved to the city. He isn't there to size her up or gloat — he just wants to be a good neighbor. Agnes, warm as she is, doesn't actually have many close friends, and the ones she does have all turned out to be supernatural-adjacent one way or another. Jacques has nothing to do with any of that — just an ordinary baker — and for the first time, Agnes finds herself building a relationship that's completely ordinary.

## Characters

- **Jacques** — French, owns the neighborhood's new bakery. Not a rival in practice; comes by simply to be neighborly.
