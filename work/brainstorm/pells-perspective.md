# Pell's Life — Kitchen to Archive

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`. Would sit alongside `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md` as a background piece rather than a present-day arc.*

## Premise

Sable and Aldric's early history, retold from Pell's side of it: from the day he finds a feral, nameless ten-year-old stealing from the chapterhouse kitchen, through to his death of old age in a southern archive decades later.

This isn't a new invention so much as a chance to dramatize a span `arcs/origin.md` already commits to but only summarizes. Established and not up for revision: Pell takes Sable in at ten; later takes in Aldric as well, as an orphan of the pestilence (see `world/aura-chronicle/overview.md`); is head archivist during the Maren conspiracy and the sealing ritual; survives Maren's sabotage; is exiled to a southern archive when the chapterhouse is struck from the records; dies there of old age, having managed very little correspondence with Tem after the split. Any version of this draft has to land on those beats, not around them.

## Open questions for this draft

- How much of the twenty years Sable and Aldric spend trapped, and their eventual life at the bakery, does Pell ever learn about — if anything? Canon leaves this essentially untouched.
- Tone: is this an old man's memory piece, working backward from the archive, or told forward in real time?
