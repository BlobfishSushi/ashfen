# Avery

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`. The least resolved draft in this folder — a hook, not yet an ending.*

## Premise

A blind young man, Avery, walks into the bakery. He doesn't see in the ordinary sense — he perceives weight and shape — and what he reads at the corner table isn't two typical ghosts. It's two things anchored by something identical, and one of them barely registers as a person at all, while the other carries so much anger under its skin that Avery is baffled it's managed to stay still and not hurt anyone. He's come to urge Agnes to do something before she gets hurt.

## Characters

- **Avery** — young, blind in the ordinary sense, perceives weight and shape instead. A perception type not yet accounted for in `world/perception-tiers.md`.

## Open questions for this draft

- What Avery actually is, perception-wise, and whether that's a new tier or its own separate thing entirely.
- What "do something" means to him, and whether he's right to be as alarmed as he is.
- No ending yet — this is currently just the inciting scene.
