# The Polymorphic Crystal

*Tag: `[EXPLORING]`.*

## Premise

A cave opens on the side of a mountain, sealed by a Dawnchurch ritual too old for anyone alive to read and too dangerous to leave alone. Mara brings in Sable and Aldric, posing as grad students in front of the back-line hunters who actually have to do the sealing, since the ghosts can get close to the thing without becoming its next casualty. It's a risky job, and a necessary one.

The crystal defends itself by making clones of the intruders "at maximized potential" — versions of them that show each ghost a part of themselves they don't know is there.

## Characters

No new named characters yet — this one runs on Sable, Aldric, and Mara, plus unnamed back-line hunters.
