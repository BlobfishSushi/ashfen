# Chapter 2 — critic and editor pass

## Where both agents agreed

**The chapter is structurally sound.** Neither found a reason to re-architect it.
Both said the same thing about the shape: arrival, mutual sizing-up, the ask,
the reversal is the right sequence, it runs under a thousand words, and it earns
its place. The revision needed was voice and line work.

**The hidden layer is well calibrated.** Agnes looking past Leo at the door, the
two cups, her glances at the corner table, and her "I was about to ask you the
same thing" all read as ordinary strangeness on a first pass and as a ghost
crossing over on a reread. Both agents said explicitly not to touch this.
Protect it.

**Protect the interrogation-room paragraph.** Both named *"never look like
you're watching somebody, look like you're reading, and turn a page every so
often on a count so the room believes it. It worked fine on interrogation rooms.
He was less sure about bakeries"* as the strongest voice passage in the chapter
and the benchmark the rest should match.

**Protect the ending.** "Save us both the trip" opens rather than closes. Both
said the chapter ends in the right place.

## The critic's headline finding

**Leo was a camera, not a person.** The observation was competent and costless.
The voice notes say *write the cost, not the cleverness*, and the chapter was
writing the cleverness: verdict-fragments about his own process, a smart-PI
generalization about bakers and informants, and no moment where being out of
leads or being fired for being right registered as weight. A man eleven months
into the case that cost him his job does not sit in a bakery making wry
observations about his own decision-making.

Applied, without adding bulk:

- *"Something like it"* → *"Long year."* The old line was any protagonist in any
  mystery. The new one is guarded, literal, and carries the eleven months.
- Added one sentence where he commits: *"A year ago he'd have laughed at a
  county clerk sending him to a baker."* That is the cost, in his register, with
  no explanation of the feeling attached.
- *"He followed the line of it twice"* → *"three times before he made himself
  stop."* Turns cataloguing into compulsion in five words.

## Applied line work

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| Three bolted-on "like X" clauses on Agnes, against a one-per-scene limit | Kept the price-quoting comparison; cut *"like she was weighing how much of an answer one visit was worth"* and *"like the sentence explained itself"* |
| Triad in a dialogue tag: *"flat, no satisfaction in it, the same tone she'd use to quote him a price"* | → *"She said it the way she'd quote him a price."* One beat |
| Scene-final button: *"and turned for the ovens before he could tell her it didn't"* | → *"She turned for the ovens."* The reader supplies his bafflement |
| Redundant pair: *"without looking at it"* / *"without counting them"* | Kept the oven timer, cut the pastry count |
| *"nobody was sitting at"* in the room list, then *"nobody within six feet"* later | Cut the first; the second look now gets to discover it |
| Explanatory tail: *"and eleven months doing this for a living had taught him those two weren't as far apart as they sounded"* | Cut. The either/or is Leo; the tail was the narrator |
| Novelistic narration: *"Leo had spent a career learning what people did with their eyes when they thought nobody was counting"* | Cut. Redundant with the interrogation-room paragraph and not in his register |
| Aphorism: *"which was usually how he decided things"* | Cut |
| Tense-conversion artifact: *"Out of leads was out of leads"* | → *"is out of leads."* Maxims stay present tense inside past-tense free indirect narration |
| Room catalog buried the tea clue among five equivalent items | Redistributed; *"The corner table had two cups on it"* now ends the paragraph on its own |
| Cold-lifting beat too buried to register | Given its own paragraph: *"First time since fall."* Still rationalized away as the ovens |
| Agnes unnamed for most of the chapter, then suddenly named | Grounded early: *"Barbara had said Agnes. There wasn't anybody else working."* |

## Considered and declined

- **The negation pair *"Not at him. Past him."*** Flagged as tic #7, but it is
  Leo clipping and self-correcting mid-observation, and it is the only instance
  in the chapter. Kept.
- **Agnes's *"Depends what you're bringing me"* as genre furniture.** Fair, but
  low cost, and rewriting her lines this early risks over-tuning a voice that is
  working. Left alone.
- **The critic's note on Agnes's warmth thinning after the opening.** Real, but
  guardedness is correct for a first meeting. Worth watching in later chapters
  rather than fixing here.

## One thing for a later chapter

The critic flagged that *MUNROE, G.* on the file tab is concealed dramatic irony
— the ghost whose file Leo is reading is at the next table — but the circuit
only closes on reread if chapter 3 names him as **Geoff Munroe**, not just
Geoff. Chapter 3 currently does. Keep it that way.
