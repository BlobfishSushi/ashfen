# Book 1 Opening — Chapter Breakdown

How the first stretch of prose actually gets written, once drafting starts. This is chapter-level, one step down from `serial-order.md`, which stays arc-level.

## The structural call this builds on

Book 1's reader-facing opening is **not** the same as its arc-level order. `arcs/index.md` still fixes Origin and The Bakery/Agnes as arcs 1 and 2, chronologically first, and nothing here changes that — they still happened first, in that order, in-world. What changes is presentation: the reader's actual chapter one opens on **Leo**, not on a 400-year-old sacrifice. Origin and Bakery/Agnes's material gets folded in through what Leo learns, rather than dramatized as two chapters the reader sits through before meeting him.

Why: Origin is dense, mythic, unfamiliar-world backstory — church politics, a corrupted ritual, four centuries of remove. Leo's case is a grounded, contemporary hook (ex-cop, cold case, a rumor about a bakery) that's a much easier door in. The heavier backstory lands better once the reader already has a reason to care who Sable and Aldric are.

Practically: **Leo Castello — Introduction** (arc 3) and **Museum Theft Aftermath** (arc 4) are being treated as one continuous chapter-level unit — arc 4's own content is thin (the heist's public fallout, the bell staying hidden, the replica), so it reads as this unit's tail rather than needing its own separate chapter block.

## Decided: how Origin surfaces here

Only what the case needs: Sable and Aldric were clerics, which is where their occult knowledge and their command of the bell come from. That's it. Maren, the sacrifice, the twenty years trapped — none of it comes up. Leo isn't told, and doesn't ask past what he can't help asking; he's got a mountain of questions and no real access yet, which is the point. The two of them read to him (and the reader) as old, capable, a little strange, and strikingly unbothered by their own circumstances — dead young, in robes, and utterly chill about it, which is its own kind of unsettling for someone meeting them cold.

**The full story is a later payoff, not a Book 1 one.** Sable and Aldric tell Leo the whole thing themselves — Maren, the door, all twenty years — once he's genuinely part of the crew, well past this opening arc. No chapter or arc assigned yet; flagging it here as a tracked setup so it doesn't get accidentally spent early or forgotten.
