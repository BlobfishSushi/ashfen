## The split

Originally planned and drafted as one chapter. **Split by the author into two:**

- **Chapter 5** — beats 21–27. Finding the museum, recon, the plan, Agnes's picks, and the wait. Ends the night of the job, before they leave, on Agnes handing Leo better towels.
- **Chapter 6** — beats 28–32. The theft and the bakery afterward.

The cut lands between the preparation and the street. Chapter 5 ends with them ready; chapter 6 opens on Dowd Street at eleven forty.

**Neither chapter is titled.** Per the author, chapter names are their call and Claude shouldn't invent them (now a standing rule in `claude/CLAUDE.md`). Draft headings are plain `# Chapter N`.

### Author decisions

- **Scope.** Two chapters. Straight chronological, present tense, Leo POV throughout. No flashback structure.
- **The museum is in Baustin.** Small, underfunded, municipal, mixed collection. Working name **the Halbrook**, on Dowd, four blocks off the river, in a former savings and loan. (The Ashfen-town alternative was considered and rejected.)
- **The complication is two-part, and neither part is a guard.**
  1. **The bell is dangerous to Sable and Aldric**, so the job runs under a standing rule that it must not sound anywhere near them. **The crew plans for this rather than getting lucky: they bring cloth and tape and wrap the clapper before the bell moves at all.** They still have to be careful the whole way out. The bell does **not** ring in these chapters.
  2. **It's heavier than anyone planned for.** Not unliftable. Just enough to wreck an exit built around a man who can move at speed.
- **The bell's real cost gets paid later**, in the chapter where they banish the wraith. Nothing in these chapters spends it.
- **The Agnes/ghosts backstory lands in chapter 5**, in the downtime before the job: salt round, warehouse, bread.
- **Agnes is on site** and picks locks, unexpectedly and very well. Her only explanation is that she was wild in her younger days, and she does not elaborate, now or in this book. *(Now in `kb/characters/agnes.md` and `kb/open-questions.md`, flagged do-not-close.)*
- **Recon is split:** one dramatized report scene, then the rest compressed.
- **Chapter 6 ends at the bakery**, bell on the table, four of them looking at it.

### Canon settled during these chapters

**The bell's effect on Sable and Aldric — corrected by the author.** Earlier drafting had a ring being effectively lethal. That is wrong. A ring is an injury, not annihilation: the ghost equivalent of being hit by a truck. Badly hurt, laid out, long recovery, useless to anybody meanwhile. **Severity scales with proximity** — across a room is survivable and awful, standing beside it is worse, holding it is unknown.

**The two branches' bells — established by the author.** The militant branch's bell (the surviving true bell) is the large two-handed one. **The archival branch carried hand bells on their belts**: about a third the size, drawn one-handed, and weaker. That's what Sable and Aldric trained on and why the true bell's weight blindsides them. It also draws the line forward to **Leo's replica**, which is an archival hand bell in shape and scale — one-handed, stuns and repels, banishes nothing.

Both now in `world/objects.md` and `world/ghost-mechanics.md`, with an open question flagged on whether an archival hand bell banishes at all or only stuns.

## The security, and why it's beatable

Worked out to satisfy `arcs/museum-theft-aftermath.md`, which requires the theft to be unsolvable from outside and the thief to have had foreknowledge of guard routes:

- One night guard, Denny, walking a fixed route out of a logbook he leaves open on the desk, route sheet laminated and taped to the wall above it.
- Interior motion zones are **disarmed while he walks** and re-armed when he finishes. That window is the job. He says the code aloud while punching it, alone, as he has for eleven years.
- **The perimeter loop stays armed all night, but the alley service door's contact is dead** — Denny props that door open with a milk crate to take the trash out at eight and the panel never makes a sound. The ghosts watch it happen four nights running. Deferred maintenance in a museum that can't afford maintenance. *(Added in revision after the editor pass caught that the perimeter alarm had been established and then never dealt with during the entry.)*
- So the deadbolt is the only real obstacle, and Agnes opens it.
- The bell is not in a high-value gallery, because nobody knows what it is. **Its obscurity is the security hole** — the whole reason this is possible.
- Sable and Aldric track the guard in real time and relay through Agnes, the only person who hears them. The plan's entire nervous system is a baker in a dark hallway.

*(Deliberately avoids the open question in `world/ghost-mechanics.md` about ghost page-turning: nothing the ghosts learn requires touching anything. The logbook is left open, the route is taped to a wall, the code is spoken aloud, the trash door is propped in front of them.)*

## Chapter 5

**Beat 21 — The morning after.** Leo comes back on four hours of sleep. The four of them start turning "steal it" into a thing with steps, and it turns out nobody knows *which* museum. The source is a ghost who came through the bakery five years ago and mentioned a piece of iron she liked, which Sable recognized off three sentences. Finding the building is Leo's half.

*(This wrinkle isn't in the chapter 4 beats but is forced by chapter 4's own line — "last anybody heard, it's in a museum... if the last update's still good." It also rhymes with the chapter 1 records counter.)*

**Beat 22 — The scouts.** Nine days of accession lists and one phone call locate the Halbrook. Then Sable and Aldric go, and nothing in the building can see them. **Dramatized: one full report scene**, Agnes translating. Then compressed: eight more nights. *(Canon load-bearing — this is the "foreknowledge of guard routes" that `museum-theft-aftermath.md` says investigators never explained.)*

The scene's comic beat is the guard's egg salad sandwich, delivered in Agnes's flat relay register. This replaced a version where Aldric's excitement was quoted verbatim with exclamation points, which the author rejected — see `styles/prose-conventions.md`, "Relaying the ghosts."

**Beat 23 — What the bell is.** Object 61-114, Gallery Three, between a whaling harpoon and a set of surveyor's chains.

**Beat 24 — Leo builds the plan.** He doesn't know how to rob a museum, he knows how museums *get* robbed. Jobs fail on the exit, not the entry. Jobs get solved on what the thief touched, not what they took. The gear stays deliberately small: cloth, tape, a bag, gloves, Agnes's picks. No favors called in, nothing acquired that could be traced.

**Beat 25 — Agnes picks locks.** The plan hits a door the ghosts can't open and Leo can't beat. She has her own picks, worn soft at the fold, and she's practiced-good rather than hobbyist-good, and she never looks at the lock. Asked, she says she was wild in her younger days and closes the subject. Leo lets it go out loud and files it. *(Do not resolve or hint toward a resolution.)*

**Beat 26 — The wait.** The bakery, the night of. Agnes tells him how she met them: Rook's salt round in this room, the two of them hurt badly enough to run, two nights of looking, a warehouse off the freight yard on Ninth, bread. Rook following her there and putting the gun down. *(Compressed `arcs/the-bakery-agnes.md`. Rook is a name and a shotgun and nothing more at this point.)*

Function: Leo's reason to trust them, the reader's first proof the ghosts can be hurt, and the setup that makes the rule land instead of arriving cold.

**Beat 27 — The rule, and the cloth.** It must not sound while either of them is near it, and it doesn't sort out what it's pointed at. Leo asks how bad, and gets the corrected answer: hit by a truck, worse the closer they are, still here afterward but laid out for a long time. Aldric says he's had worse, which is a lie, and Agnes says so.

Then the practical answer, which is Leo's: wrap the clapper before the bell moves an inch. Agnes swaps his stiff rag for flour-sack towels, which is the chapter's last line.

<hidden>Sable and Aldric are both weighing something neither says: they are asking a man they met days ago to carry the only object in the world that can put them down, with both of them too far off to help. Leo doesn't have the context to read it yet.</hidden>

## Chapter 6

**Beat 28 — Entry.** Dowd Street, eleven forty. The sidearm stays in the glovebox, because burglary with a firearm is a different statute. Agnes on the door in forty-four seconds, then three seconds inside it waiting on a panel that says nothing.

**Beat 29 — The floor.** Moving on the ghosts' timing, relayed through Agnes. The case. The cloth going on before anything else, taped, with Leo's hands inside the bell listening to the building for a count of five.

**Beat 30 — The weight.** It moves an inch and declines. Aldric works out why about a second too late: *he says oops.* Then the branch difference — theirs were hand bells, worn on the belt, about a third the size — which is the chapter's one piece of new canon and the seed of Leo's future replica. Two arms, planted, no free hands, and every timing in the plan built by a man who assumed otherwise.

**Beat 31 — The clumsy exit.** The set piece. The four steps between Three and Two take longer than the entire walk in. The ghosts won't come near the doorway with it in his arms, so Agnes has to relay from sixty feet. The tape shifts halfway through Gallery Two and she re-seats it by feel, and it costs eleven seconds, and he counts every one. No ring, no alarm, no near-miss with a person. The danger is entirely the object and the clock.

**Beat 32 — Out, and the bakery.** Twelve oh one, five minutes before Denny writes down his finish time. Agnes relocks the deadbolt. No damage anywhere, which is the part Leo keeps coming back to.

Then the bakery at half past midnight, the bell on a folded towel on a table, and the question the next chapter has to answer: how do two ghosts teach a man to ring the one thing they can't be in the room for?

## Revision history

**Editor pass, applied.** Fixed: the unaddressed perimeter alarm (see The security, above); a line where Leo perceived the ghosts' voices directly; a present-tense flash-forward that broke close-third POV; a vague hedge ("his back tells him something about itself"); two metaphors in one scene during the heist; ambiguous "they" in two transitions; lockpick timings reading backwards.

**Author pass, applied after it.** Three reversals and corrections:

1. **The flat-declarative-question "rule" is struck.** A revision had restored two or three of Leo's questions to flat periods on the strength of a rule in `prose-conventions.md` claiming that was his cop-interview register. The author confirmed that rule was never theirs — it had been generalized from a habit in the chapter 1–4 drafts and written into the style file as canon, then cited back by the editor pass as justification for keeping it. All questions now take question marks.
2. **Aldric's verbatim exclamation-point quotes are out.** They reached for emotion instead of letting Agnes's deadpan relay produce it. His lines are back in her flat register, carried by one absurdly specific detail each (egg salad; "he says oops").
3. **Chapter titles removed.** Now a standing project rule.

## Continuity constraints these chapters must not break

- **Leo cannot see or hear Sable and Aldric.** Everything from them reaches him through Agnes, all chapter, including mid-heist. No exceptions (`world/perception-tiers.md`).
- The replica does not exist yet. It gets made after this arc.
- Sable and Aldric can't touch anything: no opening the case, carrying the bell, holding a door, or turning a page.
- The bell stays a two-handed, planted-feet object from here on (`world/objects.md`).
- The theft has to end up unsolvable from the outside (`arcs/museum-theft-aftermath.md`).
- Origin stays off the page. No Maren, no sacrifice, no twenty years (`book1-opening-chapters.md`).
- No Christian oaths. No single faith consolidated in this world's history (`world/setting.md`).
