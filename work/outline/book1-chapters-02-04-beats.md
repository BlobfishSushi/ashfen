## Chapter 2 — The daytime visit

**Beat 6 — Arrival.** Leo goes to the bakery in the late afternoon. It's empty. He's lucky: a few pastries left, in addition to the sourdough.

**Beat 7 — Clocked first.** Agnes says welcome, but her brow furrows, subtle, and she looks behind him a beat too long. Leo notices. Says nothing about it. (This is Agnes clocking Geoff's ghost, trailing Leo — she doesn't yet know why there's a ghost with him, only that there is one.)

**Beat 8 — Sizing each other up.** Leo talks with Agnes, gets some general sense of the place. Agnes is warm and welcoming but doesn't reveal much about herself. He buys a pastry, the sourdough, a coffee, sits down with his case files open, ostensibly working. What he's actually doing is watching Agnes: resourceful woman, or some kind of informant?

He clocks two cups of cold tea on the corner table, clearly sitting for hours, that Agnes hasn't cleared. Doesn't look like anyone's sitting there. *(This is the standing corner-table detail — cf. the tea-ritual idea in `work/brainstorm/seeds.md`. Same ongoing thing Agnes does for Sable and Aldric; that seed can still happen independently later with a different regular noticing it.)*

Agnes keeps glancing toward the corner table, not casual, like she's tracking something there. *(Revised by the author — replaces an earlier version of this beat where Agnes paused her humming instead; the humming is cut.)*

<hidden>What's actually happening, invisible to Leo: Geoff is at the corner table, telling Agnes, Sable, and Aldric everything that happened to him — the wraith, the crash, all of it. Sable and Aldric do what they've done for other ghosts before him: calm him down, walk him through what happened, and let him know that since his business is settled now, there's a door with a warm light he can choose to walk through, to wherever comes next. Neither of them knows what's actually on the other side; it's reassurance, not a guarantee — see `world/ghost-mechanics.md`, "Crossing over," now written up as an established, repeatable thing they do, not a one-off for Geoff. Geoff goes. This is where and when Agnes actually learns what she'll tell Leo that night (Beat 12's "she just learned it all today" is this moment). Not shown from any angle but Leo's, same technique as the chapter 1 cold motif — the reader gets only what Leo could piece together, which at this point is nothing.</hidden>

Partway through, Leo notices he's stopped feeling cold. Unzips his coat for the first time in months, without connecting it to anything. *(This is the payoff of the chapter 1 "unregistered tell" — the cold was Geoff's presence the whole time; now that he's moved on, it's gone. Leo notices the change itself now, for the first time, but has no way yet to know what it means.)*

Leo's assessment of Agnes, watching her over his case files: weird woman, but he's intrigued.

**Beat 9 — He decides to ask.** Finishes his coffee. Out of leads anyway. Goes up to Agnes, asks if he can come back after closing — heard from Barbara that Agnes helps with problems, wants to ask about his. Agnes smiles: she was just about to ask him some questions herself. Leo has no idea why she'd suspect that. Files it away, doesn't press.

Chapter ends here, or on Leo leaving for the day — external pull is entirely "what happens tonight."

## Chapter 3 — After closing

**Beat 10 — The pitch.** Leo comes back at night, after close. Agnes sits him down with another coffee. He explains: a string of car accidents, same mountain stretch road, every victim had been to Mullerfield, the latest is a kid named Geoff, local to the city, and Leo's fully out of leads.

**Beat 11 — The reveal, setup.** Agnes sits, takes a breath, tells him what she's about to say will sound crazy but is true.

**Beat 12 — What Agnes knows.** She gives him specifics: Geoff's full name, Geoff Munroe; he was headed to Mullerfield for the state fair; died on the stretch off exit 43; toadstool mushrooms were growing near the tree he hit. *(Clarified by the author: this has nothing to do with wraith mechanics. It's a detail Geoff himself remembers and offered up — scared, and this is one small good thing he's holding onto out of everything bad. Agnes passes it on specifically because it's the kind of thing no civilian, and no friend of Geoff's, could plausibly have known — it's her proof, not a clue. Not a wraith-tell.)*

Leo asks if she knew Geoff, and how she possibly knows about the mushrooms. Agnes: not really, she just learned it all today. Geoff told her. *(This "today" is now specifically the corner-table exchange from Beat 8 — Leo was in the room for it and never knew.)* Leo: that can't be. Agnes: it totally can. Leo doesn't buy it, has no theory for how she'd know any of this.

**Beat 13 — The check-in.** Agnes turns to open air, as if hearing something, asks "are you sure?", then says "alright." *(Reads as Agnes checking with Sable and Aldric on whether they're willing to show themselves to Leo — not a check-in with Geoff, who's already moved on by this point per Beat 8.)*

**Beat 14 — Sable and Aldric appear.** Two faint shapes, roughly mid-20s, old-fashioned robes. The young man says they can't sustain this for long, so Agnes will translate from here, but yes, everything she said is true, and yes, this is a lot. They give their names, say something like "nice to meet you, and kudos for not freaking out," and fade.

**Beat 15 — Aftermath of the reveal.** Leo is freaking out, entirely on the inside. Agnes explains: they're still here, staying visible to a non-sensitive just costs too much to sustain, so she'll translate going forward.

*Checked against `world/ghost-mechanics.md` and `world/perception-tiers.md`: this holds up cleanly. Baseline ghost ability already includes turning visible "with effort" — reading that as effort scaling with who's meant to see it (costly against a non-sensitive like Leo, unsustainable for long) isn't a stretch of the existing rule, it's a specific case of it. No fix needed here.*

## Chapter 4 — The wraith, and the bell

**Beat 16 — What Geoff knew.** Through Agnes, Sable and Aldric relay what Geoff told them: a wraith is working that stretch of road, causing the crashes, and it's probably still there. *(Geoff himself already moved on during the Beat 8 exchange — this beat is Leo learning secondhand what Geoff said, not a live scene with Geoff. He'd been trailing Leo for months before that.)*

**Beat 17 — The actual problem.** Now the four of them have to figure out how to deal with a wraith. Leo's still reeling, doesn't know what to make of any of it or what comes next.

**Beat 18 — The bell.** Sable and Aldric: a bell might give them a real shot, but they can't touch anything themselves. The last one anyone knows of is sitting in a museum, catalogued as some kind of unknown religious relic. *(Checked against `world/objects.md`: this tracks. There's exactly one true bell left, currently in a museum, not yet stolen at this point in the story — the theft is what `arcs/leo-castello-introduction.md` says happens later in this same arc. Sable and Aldric already know how to ring it correctly; what they can't do is touch it, and there's no emitter yet to fix that — the emitter doesn't exist until a Book 2 arc. So the bell has to be rung by a living hand no matter what, and that's it: not a contradiction, just the reason Leo has to be the one who learns.)*

**Beat 19 — Leo checks the shortcut.** He asks: if they get the bell, does that crack it? Answer: not quite — somebody still has to learn to actually ring it, meaning him, since Sable and Aldric can't touch it themselves — but yes, a real step closer.

**Beat 20 — The decision.** Leo doesn't hesitate. He suggests they all go steal it from the museum, and he'll learn the bell himself.

Chapter likely ends here, or carries into the start of planning the heist — not yet detailed.
