*Book 1, opening beats — captures direction as the author gave it. Untagged = stated by the author. `<hidden>` = true in-world but not yet surfacing in Leo's POV prose. Beats below likely span more than one actual chapter; chaptering is still open, see `book1-opening-chapters.md`.*

## Beat 1 — Records counter

Leo hits an official dead end trying to get back into his case through normal channels. The clerk he deals with is **Barbara** — the same one who later points him at the bakery (see Beat 5), not a new character introduced there.

## Beat 2 — The case

A string of car accidents on the mountain stretch near **exit 43**. Leo thinks they're connected. Other cops call it bad luck: that road is dark, accidents happen there, nothing to see. Leo doesn't buy the shared explanation but doesn't have a better one yet either — he hasn't cracked what actually connects the victims.

<hidden>The true cause is a wraith working that stretch. It targets people who visited **Mullerfield**, a town, at some point before driving that road. Leo doesn't know this yet. Neither does the reader, on the page — this stays hidden until it's earned.</hidden>

## Beat 3 — Why he got pushed out

Insubordination, specifically: going to the victims' families for information after being told to drop it, and digging into the victims' pasts looking for what connects them. This digging is presumably what would eventually surface the Mullerfield connection — he's not there yet.

## Beat 4 — The ghost that follows him

**Geoff**, a college student, one of the accident victims, visiting Mullerfield for the state fair before he died. His ghost has attached itself to Leo, since he's the last person still looking into what happened. Geoff saw the wraith before he died and knew his death wasn't an accident. He's terrified, and what he wants is simple: get what he knows to someone who can actually do something about it. Clean unfinished business (`world/ghost-mechanics.md`, trigger 2).

**Resolved — how this reads on the page:** Leo doesn't perceive this at all, not even as a tell he'd recognize as one. It's implicit only — he's been running cold for months and bundling up without registering it as strange anymore, just a thing about himself he's stopped noticing. Nothing on the page confirms it's Geoff until much later.

*Possible dramatic-irony beat, not yet decided: Leo may already know Geoff's name and that he's one of the victims, from digging into the case, without any idea his ghost is the reason Leo's cold all the time. Worth deciding how much of that surfaces in ch. 1 versus staying purely a detail for a later reread.*

## Beat 5 — Barbara's recommendation

Barbara is a regular at the bakery. Tells Leo to go: he looks like hell and could use the bread, genuinely meant. Adds that if anything's bothering him, Agnes has helped people in the neighborhood before, some of it hard to explain — finding missing cats, turning up old ledgers and lost information, "things of that sort." Downplayed, mundane-sounding examples; she's not claiming anything supernatural.

## Beat 6 — The daytime visit

Leo goes to the bakery on **Lemel Street** during the day, ostensibly for bread, actually to get a look at Agnes.

## Beat 7 — Clocked first

Unbeknownst to Leo: Agnes, Sable, and Aldric make him before he says a word, via the ghost trailing him.

## Beat 8 — The arrangement

Leo asks if he can come back after closing. Agnes: she was just about to ask.

## Chaptering

Beats 1–5 = chapter 1: ordinary world, the case, the ghost (unregistered), the tip, ending on Leo heading for Lemel Street. Beats 6–8 open chapter 2, running into whatever "pitching the case to Agnes" looks like once he's back after closing (not yet planned). Proceeding on this split since it wasn't objected to; easy to fold together later if it reads better as one chapter once there's prose to look at.
