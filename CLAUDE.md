# Ashfen Chronicles

Present-day urban supernatural fiction. A bakery crew led by two 400-year-old ghosts investigates hauntings, entities, and cases that arrive because the bakery has a reputation. Horror by absence rather than spectacle.

## Project layout

```
kb/                     Knowledge base (durable canon)
  vocab.md              Canonical terms
  premise.md            Premise and genre positioning
  world/                World rules, objects, factions, church
  characters/           Cast by scope (crew, arc-conditional, historical, alt-timeline)
  arcs/                 Arc index + individual arc files by phase
    foundation/         Ordered foundation arcs
    post-foundation/    Post-foundation arcs (internal order only)
  timeline.md           Chronological backbone
  open-questions.md     Collected unresolved items
work/                   Current tasks, drafts, brainstorms (ephemeral)
source/                 Original reference files (read-only archive)
```

## Conventions

- Canon confidence uses the event log's tag system: `[CORE]`, `[CORE PLACEHOLDER]`, `[BRANCH]`, `[ALT-TIMELINE]`, `[BACKGROUND]`.
- Open questions are marked `[Open: ...]` inline and also collected in `kb/open-questions.md`.
- The emitter is the hinge for six arcs. In any timeline without it, those arcs do not happen as written.
- Foundation arcs are ordered; post-foundation arc order is undecided between arcs, fixed within arcs.
- "Ashfen" is used for the crypt and as the series title; whether it names the city is open.

## Naming

- The watchmaker is Horatio Martinez. Not Horatio Bates.
- The vampire operative is Daniel Vega. Distinct from Cora's murdered partner Daniel (no surname given).
- The mill consortium leader is Mort Voss. Distinct from the twins' father Martin Dormanescu.
- The frightened vampire is Liza. Distinct from the twins' mother Elena Dormanescu.
- The third captured hunter is Benny.
- The turned-against-his-will vampire is Greg.
- The study and the library are the same room (Alegrove arc).
